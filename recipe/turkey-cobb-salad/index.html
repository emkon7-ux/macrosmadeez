Turkey Cobb Salad

The classic diner salad with lean turkey, egg and avocado in neat rows.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Chop the romaine and spread it on a plate.

  2. Line up the turkey, egg, avocado, bacon and tomatoes in rows.

  3. Crumble a little blue cheese over (optional).

  4. Drizzle with the yogurt ranch.

TipLining the toppings up in rows makes it look like the real deal.
