Lighter Lu Rou Fan

Braised pork over rice, made with lean pork.

    5 ozLean pork loin, cut into 1-inch cubes

    1Low-sodium soy sauce

    1Five spice
  • 5

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the pork, then add the soy, five spice and water to cover.

  2. Simmer covered 45 minutes, adding the peeled boiled egg for the last 15.

  3. Cook the rice.

  4. Spoon the pork and sauce over the rice with the egg.
