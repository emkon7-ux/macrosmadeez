Lighter Chicken Pad Thai

Rice noodles with chicken, egg, sprouts and peanuts.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Soak the rice noodles in hot water for 8 minutes and drain.

  2. Stir-fry the chicken, push it aside and scramble the egg, then add noodles and tamarind sauce.

  3. Toss with bean sprouts and top with peanuts and lime.
