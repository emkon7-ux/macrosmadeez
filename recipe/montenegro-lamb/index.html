Lamb in Milk

Slow-cooked lamb with potatoes in milk.

1 serving

  • 1
    5 ozLean boneless leg of lamb, trimmed and cubed
  • 2
    1Potato
  • 3
    1/2 cupLow-fat (1%) milk

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Brown the lamb in an ovenproof pot for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the chunked potato, the milk and a pinch of salt.

  3. Cover and bake 75 minutes until tender.

  4. Serve.
