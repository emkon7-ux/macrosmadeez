Protein French Toast

French toast that actually keeps you full. The whey goes right into the batter.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 2 slicesWhole grain bread
  • ½ cupEgg whites
  • ½ scoopVanilla whey
  • ½ tspCinnamon
  • ½ cupBerries

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the egg whites, whey and cinnamon.

  2. Dip each slice and let it soak for a second.

  3. Cook on a hot pan till both sides are golden.

  4. Then pile on the berries.

Tipuse day-old bread so it soaks up the batter without falling apart.
