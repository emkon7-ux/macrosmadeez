Baked Draniki

Belarusian potato pancakes with skyr.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Grate the potato and onion and squeeze out the liquid.

  2. Mix with the egg and a pinch of salt.

  3. Spoon small pancakes onto a lined, oiled tray and bake 20 minutes, flipping once.

  4. Serve with skyr.

TipBaking saves lots of oil.
