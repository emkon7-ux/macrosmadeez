Why it's good for you

  • Healthy monounsaturated fats from avocado support heart health.
  • Milk and yogurt add calcium and 22g of protein.
  • Avocado's fiber keeps blood sugar steady.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Scoop the avocado into a blender.

  2. Add milk, Greek yogurt, honey and ice and blend until silky.

  3. Serve right away so it stays bright green.
