Fish Pepper Soup

A fiery, aromatic broth with chunks of fish.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the spices, onion and chili in the water.

  2. Add the fish.

  3. Cook gently for 8 minutes.

  4. Serve hot with herbs.
