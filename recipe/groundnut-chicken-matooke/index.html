Groundnut Chicken Stew

Chicken in a creamy peanut-tomato sauce with steamed green bananas.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the chicken with onion.

  2. Add the tomatoes and water and simmer for 20 minutes.

  3. Stir in the peanut butter.

  4. Serve with steamed plantain.

TipMatooke is traditional, but green plantain is easier to find.
