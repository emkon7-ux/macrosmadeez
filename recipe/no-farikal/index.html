Fårikål

Lamb and cabbage stew with peppercorns.

  • 3

  • 4
    1Potato

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Layer the lamb and cabbage wedges in a pot, scattering peppercorns and salt between layers.

  2. Add a cup of water, cover and simmer gently for 2 hours.

  3. Serve with boiled potatoes.
