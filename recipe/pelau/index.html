Chicken Pelau

Caramelized chicken with pigeon peas and rice.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    5 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2
    1/3 cupLong-grain white rice
  • 3
    1/2 cupPigeon peas
  • 4
    1 tspBrown sugar

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat a little oil in a pot and melt the brown sugar until dark amber, about 2 minutes.

  2. Add the cubed chicken and stir until coated and browned, 5 minutes.

  3. Add the rice, pigeon peas, 2/3 cup water and salt.

  4. Cover and cook on low 20 minutes until the rice is tender.

TipThe caramel gives the color.
