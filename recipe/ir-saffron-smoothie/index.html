Why it's good for you

  • Saffron has been studied for mood benefits.
  • Pistachios add protein and fiber.
  • 22g of protein.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Bloom the saffron in a spoon of hot water.

  2. Blend with the pistachios, banana, yogurt and ice.

  3. Top with chopped pistachios.
