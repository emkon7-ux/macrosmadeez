Kewa Datshi

Potato and cheese stew.

    2 ozLight cheese

    1Green chili (Indian or serrano)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the sliced potato in 1 cup water for 10 minutes.

  2. Add the slit chili.

  3. Stir in the cheese until melted.

  4. Serve with rice.
