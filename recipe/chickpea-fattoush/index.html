Chickpea Fattoush

A crunchy herb salad with toasted pita chips, chickpeas and sumac dressing.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toast the pita until crisp, then break it up.

  2. Chop the veggies and herbs.

  3. Whisk the lemon, oil, sumac and garlic.

  4. Toss everything together right before serving.
