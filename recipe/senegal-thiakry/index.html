Thiakry

Millet couscous with sweet yogurt.

1 serving

  • 1
    1/3 cupMillet couscous
  • 2
    3/4 cupPlain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3
    1 tspVanilla & nutmeg

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Pour 1/3 cup boiling water over the millet couscous, cover 5 minutes and fluff.

  2. Cool, then mix with the yogurt, vanilla and nutmeg.

  3. Sweeten lightly if you like.

  4. Chill 30 minutes and serve.
