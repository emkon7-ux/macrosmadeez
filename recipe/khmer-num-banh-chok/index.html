Num Banh Chok

Rice noodles with green fish curry.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the fish in the lemongrass curry for 8 minutes and flake.

  2. Cook the rice noodles.

  3. Pour the curry over the noodles.

  4. Top with fresh vegetables and herbs.
