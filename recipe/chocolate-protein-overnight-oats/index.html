Chocolate Protein Overnight Oats

Make it at night, grab it in the morning. Tastes like chocolate pudding.

High proteinVegetarianBreakfast
380Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
38g
Fat
9g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • ½ cupRolled oats
  • 1 scoopChocolate whey
  • ¾ cupAlmond milk
  • ⅓ cupGreek yogurt

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Add the oats to a jar.

  2. Stir in the whey.

  3. Pour in the milk and the yogurt, and mix.

  4. Then pop it in the fridge overnight.

Tipmake three jars on Sunday and breakfast is done till Wednesday.
