Canjeero with Eggs

Somali spongy pancakes with scrambled eggs.

1 serving

    2Canjeero pancakes
  • 2
    2Eggs
  • 3
    1/2 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Warm the canjeero in a dry pan.

  2. Scramble the eggs and whites with salt.

  3. Serve together.

  4. Add tea on the side if you like.

TipCanjeero is like a thin injera.
