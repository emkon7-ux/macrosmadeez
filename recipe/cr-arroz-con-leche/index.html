Protein Arroz con Leche

Rice pudding with cinnamon and protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    1/4 cupLong-grain white rice
  • 2
    1 cupLow-fat (1%) milk
  • 3
    1/2 scoopVanilla or unflavored whey protein powder
  • 4
    1Cinnamon

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the rice in the milk with a pinch of salt, stirring, for 25 minutes until creamy.

  2. Cool 5 minutes.

  3. Stir in the protein powder.

  4. Dust with cinnamon.
