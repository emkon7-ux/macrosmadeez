Onban (Warm Rice Soup)

Rice in chicken broth with mushrooms.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chicken in 3 cups broth for 15 minutes; shred.

  2. Add the sliced mushrooms.

  3. Spoon the cooked rice into a bowl and ladle over the broth.

  4. Top with the chicken.
