Garudhiya

A clear tuna broth with lime and chili.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the tuna in 2 cups salted water for 10 minutes.

  2. Add the sliced onion and chili.

  3. Cook 10 minutes more.

  4. Serve with lime and rice.
