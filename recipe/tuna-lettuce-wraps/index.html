Tuna Lettuce Wraps

No-cook lunch that's creamy, crunchy and packed with protein. Greek yogurt does everything mayo does, with way more protein.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinner
250Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
10g

7g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 canTuna, drained
  • ⅓ cupGreek yogurt (not mayo)
  • 1 stalkCelery, diced
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • 4 leavesButter lettuce

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. First, drain the tuna really well.

  2. Stir in the yogurt and the Dijon.

  3. Fold in the celery.

  4. Then spoon it into the lettuce cups.

Tippat the lettuce dry so the wraps don't get soggy.
