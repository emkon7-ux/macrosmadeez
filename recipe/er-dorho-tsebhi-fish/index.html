Asa Tsebhi (Fish Stew)

Red Sea fish in berbere sauce.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Cook the onion slowly until jammy, then add garlic, berbere and tomato.

  2. Simmer the sauce 10 minutes with a splash of water.

  3. Lay the fish in, cover and cook 8 minutes until it flakes.
