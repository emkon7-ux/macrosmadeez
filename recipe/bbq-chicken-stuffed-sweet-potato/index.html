BBQ Chicken Stuffed Sweet Potato

A loaded sweet potato with tangy BBQ chicken. 40g of protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 mediumSweet potato
  • 4 ozShredded chicken
  • 2 tbspBBQ sauce
  • 1Green onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Bake or microwave the sweet potato till soft.

  2. Toss the chicken in the BBQ sauce.

  3. Split the potato and stuff it.

  4. Then top it with green onion.

Tipmicrowave the potato for 6 to 8 minutes if you're short on time.
