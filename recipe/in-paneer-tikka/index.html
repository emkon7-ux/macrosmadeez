Paneer Tikka

Grilled marinated paneer with peppers and onion.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mix the yogurt with tikka spice and coat the paneer, pepper and onion.

  2. Thread onto skewers and rest 20 minutes.

  3. Grill or broil until charred at the edges.
