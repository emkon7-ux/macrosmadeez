Chicken & Lentil Soup

Cozy soup that's actually filling. Makes 4 bowls, and macros are per bowl.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinner
350Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
40g
Fat
5g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 lbChicken breast
  • 1 cupRed lentils
  • 2Carrots, diced
  • 2 stalksCelery, diced
  • 6 cupsChicken broth

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the carrots and celery in a big pot.

  2. Add the broth and lentils.

  3. Drop in the chicken and simmer for twenty five minutes.

  4. Then shred the chicken right in the pot.

Tipred lentils melt into the broth and make it thick without any cream.
