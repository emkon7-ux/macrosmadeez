Baked Tuna Brik

A crisp pastry with tuna, egg and capers.

1 serving

    2 ozCanned tuna in water, drained
  • 2
    1Egg
  • 3
    1 sheetBrik or phyllo
  • 4
    1 tspCapers & parsley

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Lay the tuna and capers on the pastry.

  2. Crack the egg on top and fold into a triangle.

  3. Bake at 400°F for 12 minutes.

TipKeep the yolk runny if you like.
