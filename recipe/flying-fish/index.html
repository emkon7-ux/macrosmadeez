Bajan Flying Fish

Seasoned fish with a light creole sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Rub the fish with the Bajan seasoning and a pinch of salt.

  2. Pan-sear in a little oil 2 to 3 minutes per side.

  3. Warm the creole sauce in a small pan for 5 minutes.

  4. Spoon the sauce over the fish.

TipNormally with cou-cou.
