Shrimp Summer Rolls

Fresh, crunchy rice paper rolls with a light peanut-lime dip.

1 serving

    5 ozCooked shrimp, halved

    4Rice paper sheets
  • 3
    1 ozRice vermicelli
  • 4
    1 cupLettuce & carrot & cucumber
  • 5
    1 handfulMint & cilantro

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Dip the rice paper in warm water for 5 seconds.

  2. Lay down the shrimp, then the noodles, veggies and herbs.

  3. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.

  4. Serve with the peanut-lime sauce.

TipPut the shrimp down first so they show through the wrapper.
