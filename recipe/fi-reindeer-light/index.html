Sautéed Venison with Lingonberries

Lean venison with mash and berries.

    4 ozPotato
  • 3
    1 tbspLingonberries

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Thinly slice the venison and sauté in a hot pan for 4 minutes.

  2. Boil and mash the potato with salt.

  3. Season the meat.

  4. Serve with the mash and lingonberries.
