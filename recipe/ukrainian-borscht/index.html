Ukrainian Borscht

Ukraine's beet soup with beef and beans.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the beef in 4 cups salted water for 20 minutes.

  2. Add the grated beets, shredded cabbage and cubed potato and cook 25 minutes.

  3. Add the beans and heat through.

  4. Serve with yogurt.
