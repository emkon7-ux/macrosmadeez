Grilled Lemon Chicken (Poulet Mayo)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon, garlic and onion.

  2. Grill until charred.

  3. Mix the yogurt and mustard.

  4. Serve the chicken with the sauce.
