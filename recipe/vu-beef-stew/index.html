Santo Beef Stew

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the cubed beef in a pot for 6 minutes.

  2. Add the sliced onion and chunked taro with water to cover.

  3. Stew covered 75 minutes until tender.

  4. Stir in the spinach, season and serve.
