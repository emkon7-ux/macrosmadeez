Why it's good for you

  • Jackfruit is a good source of vitamin C and fiber.
  • Natural sugars give quick energy before a workout.
  • Greek yogurt adds protein for a balanced snack.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Seed the ripe jackfruit pieces.

  2. Blend with the banana, yogurt and ice until smooth.

  3. Serve cold.
