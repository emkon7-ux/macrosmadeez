White Bean Ratatouille

Provençal stewed vegetables, made filling with creamy white beans.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Roast the eggplant, zucchini and pepper at 425°F for 20 minutes.

  2. Simmer the tomatoes, garlic and herbs.

  3. Fold in the roasted veg and beans.

  4. Simmer for 10 minutes and finish with basil.

TipRoasting the veg first keeps it from turning mushy.
