Why it's good for you

  • Banana and oats give steady energy for the morning.
  • Milk and yogurt add 24g protein and calcium.
  • Cinnamon may help steady blood sugar.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add everything to the blender.

  2. Blend until frothy.

  3. Serve with cinnamon on top.
