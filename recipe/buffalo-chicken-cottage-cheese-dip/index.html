Buffalo Chicken Cottage Cheese Dip

All the buffalo dip flavor with blended cottage cheese instead of cream cheese and ranch.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 4 ozShredded chicken
  • ½ cupCottage cheese
  • 2 tbspHot sauce
  • 1 cupVeggie sticks

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese till it's silky.

  2. Stir in the hot sauce.

  3. Fold in the chicken.

  4. Then warm it up and grab your veggies.

Tipbake it at 375 for ten minutes if you want it bubbly.
