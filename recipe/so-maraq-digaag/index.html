Maraq Digaag

Somali chicken soup with xawaash spice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Brown the chicken with the onion and xawaash.

  2. Add the tomato, potato, carrot and 4 cups water and simmer 25 minutes.

  3. Season and serve with a squeeze of lime.

TipXawaash is cumin, cardamom and cinnamon.
