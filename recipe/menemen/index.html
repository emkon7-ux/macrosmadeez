Menemen

Turkish soft-scrambled eggs cooked with tomatoes and peppers.

VegetarianGluten-freeBreakfastTürkiye15 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    2Eggs
  • 2
    1/2 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs
  • 3
    2Tomatoes, grated
  • 4
    1Green pepper, diced
  • 5
    1 tspPul biber (chili flakes)
  • 6
    1 tspOlive oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the peppers in the oil.

  2. Add the tomatoes and cook until jammy.

  3. Stir in the eggs and whites gently.

  4. Take it off the heat while it's still soft and add the chili.

TipGrating the tomatoes gives you a smooth sauce in minutes.
