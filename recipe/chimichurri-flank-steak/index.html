Chimichurri Flank Steak

Grilled flank steak with the bright, garlicky herb sauce Argentina is known for.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Stir the parsley, garlic, vinegar, oil and chili together.

  2. Season the steak and grill it hot for 4 to 5 minutes per side.

  3. Rest for 5 minutes.

  4. Slice against the grain and spoon the chimichurri over.

TipMake the chimichurri an hour early so the flavors come together.
