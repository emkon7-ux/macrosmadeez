Sharba Libya

Libya's spiced lamb and orzo soup, made with lean chicken here.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the chicken with onion and spices.

  2. Stir in the tomato paste and broth.

  3. Simmer for 20 minutes, then add the orzo and chickpeas.

  4. Finish with mint and lemon.

TipDried mint at the end is the traditional touch.
