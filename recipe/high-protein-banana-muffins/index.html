High-Protein Banana Muffins

Blender banana muffins with no flour. Makes 8, and 2 muffins = 280 cal and 22g protein.

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 2Ripe bananas
  • 1 cupRolled oats
  • ½ cupGreek yogurt
  • 1 scoopVanilla whey
  • 2Eggs

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend everything till it's smooth.

  2. Pour it into a muffin tin.

  3. Bake at 350 for about eighteen minutes.

  4. Let them cool, then grab two for breakfast.

Tipthe spottier the bananas, the sweeter the muffins.
