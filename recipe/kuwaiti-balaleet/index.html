Saffron Egg Vermicelli

Sweet vermicelli with an egg.

  • 2
    2Eggs
  • 3
    1 pinchSaffron

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the vermicelli in water with the saffron for 5 minutes; drain.

  2. Beat the eggs and cook into a thin omelet.

  3. Plate the noodles.

  4. Top with the omelet.
