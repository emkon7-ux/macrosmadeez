Chicken Maqluba

The upside-down rice dish with chicken and roasted vegetables.

1 serving

  • 1
    5 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2
    1/3 cupLong-grain white rice
  • 3
    1Eggplant & cauliflower, roasted
  • 4
    1 tspSeven-spice

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cube the eggplant and cauliflower and roast at 425°F for 20 minutes.

  2. Layer the browned chicken, the vegetables and the rinsed rice in a pot with the seven-spice.

  3. Add 2/3 cup stock, cover and cook on low 25 minutes.

  4. Rest 5 minutes, then flip out onto a plate.

TipThe flip is the fun part.
