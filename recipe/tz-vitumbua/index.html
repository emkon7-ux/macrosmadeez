Baked Vitumbua

Coconut rice cakes, cooked in a pan.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the rice flour, coconut milk, cardamom and a pinch of yeast or baking powder; rest 30 minutes.

  2. Heat a mold pan or muffin tin lightly oiled.

  3. Fill and cook 3 minutes until set underneath.

  4. Flip and brown 2 minutes.
