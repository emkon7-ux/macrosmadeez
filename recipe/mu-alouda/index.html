Why it's good for you

  • Basil seeds add fiber that keeps you full.
  • Milk and yogurt add calcium and protein.
  • Cooling and hydrating.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Soak the basil seeds in water for 10 minutes.

  2. Blend the milk, yogurt and banana.

  3. Stir in the seeds.
