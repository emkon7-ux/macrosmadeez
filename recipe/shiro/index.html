Shiro

A silky chickpea-flour stew with garlic and berbere. Comforting and quick.

VeganVegetarianGluten-freeLunch & dinnerEritrea20 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    1/3 cupShiro (spiced chickpea flour)
  • 2
    1Onion, minced
  • 3
    2Garlic cloves
  • 4
    1Tomato, grated
  • 5
    1 1/2 cupsWater

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the onion and garlic until soft.

  2. Add the tomato and water and bring to a simmer.

  3. Whisk in the shiro powder slowly.

  4. Simmer for 10 minutes, whisking, until smooth.

TipPlain chickpea flour plus berbere works if you can't find shiro.
