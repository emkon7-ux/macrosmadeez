Lighter Sarmale

Cabbage rolls with lean mince and rice.

    5 ozLean pork or turkey

  • 3
    6Sour cabbage leaves
  • 4
    1/2 cupTomato

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the meat, rice, salt and pepper.

  2. Roll spoonfuls in the cabbage leaves, tucking in the sides.

  3. Layer in a pot with the tomato and water to cover.

  4. Simmer covered 75 minutes.

TipServe with yogurt.
