One-Pan Chicken & Veggie Sheet Bake

Chop, toss, bake. Dinner's done and there's one pan to wash.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinner

Protein

Carbs

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozChicken breast, cubed
  • 1 cupBroccoli florets
  • 1Zucchini, sliced
  • ½Red onion
  • 1 tspSmoked paprika

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Chop everything into bite size pieces.

  2. Toss it with a little oil and the paprika.

  3. Spread it all out on a sheet pan.

  4. Bake at 425 for about twenty minutes.

Tipdon't crowd the pan, or the veggies steam instead of roast.
