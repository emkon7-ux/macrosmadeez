Lake Chad Grilled Capitaine

Nile perch grilled with garlic, chili and lemon.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Rub the fish with minced garlic, chopped chili and salt.

  2. Grill 4 to 5 minutes per side until it flakes.

  3. Squeeze lemon over.

  4. Serve.

TipAny firm white fish works.
