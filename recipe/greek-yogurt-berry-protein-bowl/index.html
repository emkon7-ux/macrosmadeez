Greek Yogurt Berry Protein Bowl

My go-to breakfast when I need something fast that actually keeps me full. Creamy, a little sweet, zero cooking.

High proteinVegetarianGluten-freeBreakfast

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 cupNonfat Greek yogurt
  • ½ scoopVanilla whey protein
  • ¾ cupMixed berries
  • 1 tbspChia seeds

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Okay. Scoop the yogurt into a bowl.

  2. Stir in the whey till it's super smooth.

  3. Pile those berries on top.

  4. Then sprinkle on the chia.

Tipstir the whey in before the berries so it gets totally smooth.
