Lighter Beef Rendang

Slow-cooked dry beef curry with coconut.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    7 ozLean stewing beef (top or bottom round), cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2
    1/4 cupLight coconut milk
  • 3
    2 tbspRendang paste

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Fry the rendang paste until fragrant.

  2. Add the beef and coconut milk and simmer uncovered.

  3. Cook on low for 2 hours, stirring, until dark and almost dry.
