Thai Peanut Chicken Bowl

Peanut powder makes a creamy Thai sauce for a fraction of the calories.

High proteinLunch & dinnerThailand
420Calories
Protein
42g
Carbs
38g
Fat
10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozChicken breast
  • ½ cupBrown rice
  • 1 cupShredded cabbage
  • 2 tbspPeanut butter powder
  • 1 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the peanut powder, soy and a little water.

  2. Slice the chicken.

  3. Add rice and cabbage to a bowl.

  4. Then top with chicken and drizzle the peanut sauce.

Tipadd a squeeze of lime to the sauce for extra zing.
