Misir Wot

Spicy red lentil stew with berbere. Ethiopian comfort food.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the onion slowly until soft and sweet.

  2. Add the berbere, garlic, ginger and tomato paste.

  3. Stir in the lentils and water.

  4. Simmer for 20 minutes until thick.

TipEat it with teff injera, which keeps it gluten-free.
