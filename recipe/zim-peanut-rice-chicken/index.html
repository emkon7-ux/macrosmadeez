Dovi Chicken (Peanut Stew)

Zimbabwe's peanut butter chicken stew.

  • 4
    1Onion
  • 5
    1 cupCollard greens

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cube the chicken, season, and brown in a pot for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the diced onion and tomato and cook 5 minutes.

  3. Stir in the peanut butter with 3/4 cup water and simmer 15 minutes.

  4. Add the sliced collards for the last 5 minutes until wilted. Season and serve.

TipDovi means peanut butter in Shona.
