Ping Gai

Lemongrass grilled chicken with jaew dip.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix chopped lemongrass, garlic and a pinch of salt; rub over the chicken and marinate 20 minutes.

  2. Grill 6 to 7 minutes per side until cooked through.

  3. Slice.

  4. Serve with the jaew dip and sticky rice.

TipJaew is a smoky chili dip.
