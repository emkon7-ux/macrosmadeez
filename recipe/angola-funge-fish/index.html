Calulu de Peixe

Fish and vegetable stew with greens.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the chopped tomato in a little oil for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the sliced okra, spinach and 1/2 cup water and cook 8 minutes.

  3. Add the fish.

  4. Simmer 8 minutes until it flakes.
