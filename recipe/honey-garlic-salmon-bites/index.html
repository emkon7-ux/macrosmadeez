Honey Garlic Salmon Bites

Glossy honey garlic salmon bites. Takeout flavor, healthy fats and 35g of protein.

High proteinLunch & dinner
400Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
20g
Fat
18g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozSalmon, cubed
  • 1 tbspHoney
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 1 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 cupBroccoli

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cut the salmon into bites.

  2. Sear them in a hot pan.

  3. Add the honey, garlic and soy and let it get sticky.

  4. Then serve it with broccoli.

Tippat the salmon dry so it sears instead of steams.
