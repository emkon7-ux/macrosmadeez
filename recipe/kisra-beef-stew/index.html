Okra & Beef Stew (Bamia)

Okra and lean beef braised in a spiced tomato sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with onion and garlic.

  2. Add the tomatoes, spices and water and simmer for 30 minutes.

  3. Add the okra and cook for 10 minutes.

  4. Serve with a little rice or flatbread.

TipWhole okra pods stay less slimy than sliced.
