Panama Corvina Ceviche

Sea bass cured in lime with onion and chili.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Dice the very fresh fish into small cubes.

  2. Cover with fresh lime juice and a pinch of salt.

  3. Add the finely diced onion and jalapeño.

  4. Chill 20 minutes until the fish turns opaque, then serve.
