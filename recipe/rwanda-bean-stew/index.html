Rwandan Bean Stew

Red beans simmered with tomato, onion and a little peanut.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the onion and garlic.

  2. Add the tomato, beans and water.

  3. Simmer for 20 minutes and stir in the peanut butter.

  4. Add the spinach at the end.
