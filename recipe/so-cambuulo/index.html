Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Simmer the soaked beans for about 45 minutes until tender.

  2. Stir in the corn and cook 5 minutes more.

  3. Drain, season with salt and drizzle with sesame oil.

TipOften eaten with sugar; skip it.
