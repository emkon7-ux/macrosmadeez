Encebollado

Tuna and yuca soup with pickled onions.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the cubed yuca with the chopped tomato in 2 cups salted water for 20 minutes.

  2. Add the tuna in chunks.

  3. Cook gently 5 minutes until just done.

  4. Serve topped with the pickled red onions.
