Teriyaki Chicken Donburi

A takeout-style rice bowl with a homemade lower-sugar teriyaki glaze.

High proteinLunch & dinnerJapan25 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozChicken breast, sliced
  • 2
    2/3 cupCooked short-grain rice
  • 3
    2 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
  • 4
    1 tspHoney
  • 5
    1 tspFresh ginger, grated
  • 6
    1 cupSteamed broccoli

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, ginger and a splash of water.

  2. Sear the chicken until golden and cooked through.

  3. Pour in the sauce and let it bubble until glossy.

  4. Spoon over rice with broccoli and top with sesame seeds.

TipUse chicken thigh if you want it juicier. It adds about 60 calories.
