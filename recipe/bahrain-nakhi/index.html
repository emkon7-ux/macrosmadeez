Spiced Chickpeas (Nakhi)

Boiled chickpeas with cumin and lemon.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Warm the drained chickpeas in a pan with a splash of water.

  2. Toss with cumin and salt.

  3. Squeeze lemon over.

  4. Serve warm.
