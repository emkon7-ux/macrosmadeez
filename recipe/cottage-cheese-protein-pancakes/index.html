Cottage Cheese Protein Pancakes

Blender pancakes with 35g of protein. You'd never guess there's cottage cheese in them.

High proteinVegetarianBreakfast
350Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
28g

11g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • ½ cupCottage cheese
  • 2Eggs
  • ⅓ cupRolled oats
  • ½ tspCinnamon

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend everything till it's smooth.

  2. Heat a nonstick pan on medium.

  3. Pour in little pancakes.

  4. Flip them when you see bubbles on top.

Tipkeep them small so they're easy to flip.
