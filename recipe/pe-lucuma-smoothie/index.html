Lúcuma Smoothie

Peru's caramel-tasting fruit in a protein shake.

Why it's good for you

  • Lúcuma is low glycemic and adds fiber.
  • Natural caramel taste without sugar.
  • 22g of protein.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add the lúcuma powder, banana, yogurt and milk to a blender.

  2. Blend with ice until creamy.

  3. Taste and serve right away.

TipLúcuma is Peru's favorite ice cream flavor.
