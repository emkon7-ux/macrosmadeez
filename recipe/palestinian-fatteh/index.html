Chickpea Fatteh

Toasted bread, chickpeas, garlic yogurt and pine nuts.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toast the pita and break into pieces.

  2. Warm the chickpeas with a splash of water.

  3. Layer pita, chickpeas and the garlic yogurt.

  4. Top with toasted pine nuts.
