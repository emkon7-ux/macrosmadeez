Lighter Koshari

Egypt's favorite street food: lentils, rice and pasta under a spiced tomato sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the lentils, rice and pasta.

  2. Simmer the tomatoes with garlic, cumin and a splash of vinegar.

  3. Layer the grains and chickpeas in a bowl.

  4. Top with the sauce and crispy onions.

TipBake the onions instead of frying them to save about 150 calories.
