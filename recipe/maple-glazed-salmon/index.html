Maple-Glazed Salmon

Sticky maple-Dijon glaze on roasted salmon with green beans.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    5 ozSalmon fillet
  • 2
    1 tbspPure maple syrup
  • 3
    1 tspDijon mustard
  • 4
    1 tspTamari
  • 5
    1 cupGreen beans

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the maple syrup, Dijon and tamari.

  2. Brush over the salmon on a lined tray with the green beans.

  3. Roast at 425°F for 12 minutes.

  4. Brush with more glaze and broil for 1 minute.

TipReal maple syrup only. Pancake syrup burns and tastes flat.
