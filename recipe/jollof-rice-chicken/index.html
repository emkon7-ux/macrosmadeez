Jollof Rice with Grilled Chicken

Smoky tomato-pepper rice with grilled chicken. The dish West Africa argues about.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Fry the blended pepper base until it darkens.

  2. Add the spices, stock and rice.

  3. Cover tightly and steam on low for 25 minutes.

  4. Serve with grilled spiced chicken.

TipLet the bottom catch a little. That smoky 'party jollof' flavor is the goal.
