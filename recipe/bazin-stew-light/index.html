Libyan Lamb & Tomato Stew

Lean lamb simmered in a spiced tomato sauce with potatoes and eggs.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the lamb with onion.

  2. Add the tomatoes, spices and water and simmer for 40 minutes.

  3. Add the potato and cook until tender.

  4. Serve with a halved boiled egg.

TipUse a cumin, cinnamon, turmeric and black pepper mix if you can't find bzaar.
