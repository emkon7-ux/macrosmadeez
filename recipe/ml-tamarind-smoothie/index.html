Why it's good for you

  • Tamarind adds antioxidants, magnesium and a tangy kick.
  • Banana gives energy and potassium.
  • Yogurt adds protein and probiotics.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Stir the tamarind paste into 2 tablespoons of warm water.

  2. Blend it with the banana, Greek yogurt and a handful of ice.

  3. Pour into a glass and serve right away.

TipTamarind juice is popular in Mali.
