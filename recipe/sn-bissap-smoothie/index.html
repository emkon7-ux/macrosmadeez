Why it's good for you

  • Hibiscus is rich in antioxidants and may support blood pressure.
  • Strawberries add vitamin C and fiber.
  • 18g of protein from yogurt.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Steep dried hibiscus in hot water, then chill.

  2. Blend the bissap with strawberries, yogurt and ice.

  3. Serve with a sprig of mint.
