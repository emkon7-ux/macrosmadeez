Eru with Fish

Wild greens with waterleaf and smoked fish.

1 serving

  • 1
    3 cupsEru or spinach

    3 ozSmoked mackerel (or smoked catfish), flaked and bones removed
  • 3
    1 tspPalm oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the finely sliced greens in 1/2 cup water for 15 minutes.

  2. Add the flaked fish and palm oil.

  3. Simmer 10 minutes.

  4. Season and serve.
