Barramundi with Mango Salsa

Crispy-skin barramundi with a tropical mango and avocado salsa.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Score the skin and season the fish.

  2. Sear skin-side down for 4 minutes until crisp.

  3. Flip and cook 2 more minutes.

  4. Top with the mango salsa.

TipPress the fillet down for the first 30 seconds so the skin stays flat.
