Fërgesë with Eggs

Peppers, tomatoes and cottage cheese.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Roast the peppers 15 minutes, then peel and slice.

  2. Cook them with the chopped tomatoes in a pan for 8 minutes.

  3. Stir in the cottage cheese and beaten egg with a pinch of salt.

  4. Transfer to a small dish and bake 15 minutes until set.
