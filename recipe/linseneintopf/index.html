Lentil Stew with Sausage

Linseneintopf, a German cold-weather stew with lentils and turkey sausage.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the sausage, then remove it.

  2. Soften the veggies in the same pot.

  3. Add the lentils, broth and bay leaf and simmer for 30 minutes.

  4. Return the sausage and finish with the vinegar.

TipSwap in smoked tofu to make it vegan.
