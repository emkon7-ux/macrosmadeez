Lemon Chicken Piccata

Thin chicken cutlets in a bright lemon-caper pan sauce.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozChicken breast, butterflied
  • 2
    1 tbspFlour, for dusting
  • 3
    1Lemon, juiced
  • 4
    1 tbspCapers
  • 5
    1/2 cupChicken broth
  • 6
    1 tspButter

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Pound the chicken thin and dust it with flour.

  2. Sear for 3 minutes per side, then set it aside.

  3. Simmer the broth, lemon and capers, then swirl in the butter.

  4. Return the chicken to the pan and coat it.

TipServe over zucchini noodles or a little pasta.
