Lighter Chipsi Mayai

A fries omelet. Baked fries keep it lighter.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    4 ozPotato, baked fries
  • 2
    2Eggs
  • 3
    1/2 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Bake the potato fries at 425°F for 20 minutes.

  2. Put them in a non-stick pan and pour over the beaten eggs and whites.

  3. Cook until set underneath, then flip.

  4. Cook 1 minute more and serve.
