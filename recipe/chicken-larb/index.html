Chicken Larb

Zesty minced chicken salad with lime, herbs and toasted rice, served in lettuce cups.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the chicken in a splash of water until no longer pink.

  2. Take it off the heat and add the lime, fish sauce and chili.

  3. Toss with the shallot, herbs and toasted rice.

  4. Spoon into lettuce cups.

TipToast raw rice in a dry pan, then grind it. That's the secret crunch.
