Chicken Shawarma Bowl

Your shawarma order, made at home. Trimmed thighs keep it juicy, a small scoop of rice keeps it balanced.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerLebanon

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozChicken thigh, trimmed
  • 1 tspShawarma spices
  • 1 cupCucumber-tomato salad
  • ¼ cupTzatziki
  • ⅓ cupRice

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Rub the chicken with the spices.

  2. Sear it, then slice it up.

  3. Add the rice and salad to a bowl.

  4. Then the chicken and a big spoon of tzatziki.

Tiplet the chicken sit in the spices for 30 minutes if you have time.
