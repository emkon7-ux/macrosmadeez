Chicken & Shrimp Paella

A saffron rice pan loaded with shrimp, chicken and peas.

  • 6
    1/4 cupPeas & red pepper

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the chicken, then the peppers and garlic.

  2. Add the rice, paprika and saffron broth and don't stir.

  3. Simmer for 18 minutes, then nestle in the shrimp and peas.

  4. Let a crispy crust form on the bottom.

TipThe crispy bottom (socarrat) is the best part. Listen for the crackle.
