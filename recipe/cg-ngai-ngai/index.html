Ngai Ngai with Fish

Sour roselle leaves with smoked fish.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chopped leaves with onion for 15 minutes.

  2. Add the smoked fish and palm oil.

  3. Cook 10 minutes and serve with rice.
