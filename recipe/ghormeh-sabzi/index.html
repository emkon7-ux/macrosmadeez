Ghormeh Sabzi

Iran's beloved herb stew with beef and kidney beans.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    5 ozLean stewing beef (top or bottom round), cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2
    2 cupsHerbs (parsley, cilantro, fenugreek)
  • 3
    1/2 cupKidney beans
  • 4
    2Dried limes

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Finely chop the herbs and fry in a little oil for 8 minutes until dark and fragrant.

  2. Brown the beef in a pot for 5 minutes.

  3. Add the herbs, pierced dried limes and 2 cups water and simmer covered 60 minutes, adding the beans for the last 15.

  4. Season and serve with rice.
