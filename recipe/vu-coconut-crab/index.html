Coconut Prawns

Prawns cooked in coconut and lime.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the coconut milk with a pinch of salt for 3 minutes.

  2. Add the shrimp.

  3. Cook 3 minutes until pink.

  4. Finish with a squeeze of lime.
