Bondibai

Rice pudding with cardamom.

1 serving

  • 1
    1/4 cupBasmati rice
  • 2
    1 cupLow-fat (1%) milk
  • 3
    1 pinchCardamom

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the rice in the milk, stirring, 25 minutes until soft and creamy.

  2. Add the cardamom.

  3. Sweeten lightly.

  4. Serve warm.
