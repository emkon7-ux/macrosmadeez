Barley & Chicken Bowl

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the barley in 1 cup salted water for 30 minutes.

  2. Sauté the sliced mushrooms and cubed chicken for 10 minutes.

  3. Stir into the barley.

  4. Season with salt and pepper.
