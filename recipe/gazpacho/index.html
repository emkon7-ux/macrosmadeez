Gazpacho

A chilled blended tomato soup that's like summer in a glass.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Roughly chop all the veggies.

  2. Blend with the vinegar, oil and salt until smooth.

  3. Chill for at least 1 hour.

  4. Serve topped with diced cucumber.
