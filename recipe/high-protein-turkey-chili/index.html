High-Protein Turkey Chili

One pot, four big bowls. Macros are per bowl.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 lbLean ground turkey
  • 1 canKidney beans
  • 1 canCrushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbspChili spices

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the turkey in a big pot.

  2. Stir in the spices.

  3. Add the tomatoes and the beans.

  4. Then let it simmer for twenty minutes.
