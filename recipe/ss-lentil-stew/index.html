Lentil & Pumpkin Stew

Red lentils with pumpkin and onion.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Soften the onion in a little oil with the cumin.

  2. Add the lentils, pumpkin, tomato and 3 cups of water.

  3. Simmer 20 minutes until the lentils break down and the pumpkin is soft.
