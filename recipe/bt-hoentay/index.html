Baked Hoentay

Buckwheat dumplings with greens.

    1 ozPaneer

    1 tspKashmiri chili powder

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the buckwheat flour with warm water into a soft dough.

  2. Wilt the turnip greens and mix with the cheese, chili powder and salt.

  3. Fill rounds of dough, pinch closed and steam 15 minutes.

  4. Crisp in a lightly oiled pan 2 minutes per side.
