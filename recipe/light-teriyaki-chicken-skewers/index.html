Light Teriyaki Chicken Skewers

Sweet and smoky skewers with a homemade low-sugar teriyaki. Perfect for the grill or the oven.

High proteinLunch & dinnerJapan

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozChicken breast, cubed
  • 2 tbspLow-sugar teriyaki
  • 1Bell pepper
  • ½ cupPineapple chunks

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toss the chicken in the teriyaki.

  2. Thread the chicken, peppers and pineapple.

  3. Grill for about ten minutes, turning once.

  4. Then brush on a little more sauce.

Tipsoak wooden skewers in water for 20 minutes so they don't burn.
