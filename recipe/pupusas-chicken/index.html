Chicken & Bean Pupusas

Stuffed corn cakes with curtido slaw.

    1/4 cupRefried beans

    1 cupCurtido slaw

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the masa harina with about 1/3 cup warm water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.

  2. Divide into 3 balls, flatten each, fill with chicken and beans, and seal into a disc.

  3. Cook on a hot dry griddle 3 to 4 minutes per side until spotted brown.

  4. Serve with the curtido.

TipCurtido is a tangy slaw.
