Why it's good for you

  • Pineapple contains bromelain, which can help reduce bloating.
  • Lots of vitamin C for immune support.
  • Yogurt turns it into a protein snack.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add the mango, pineapple and Greek yogurt to a blender.

  2. Blend with ice until smooth.

  3. Serve cold.
