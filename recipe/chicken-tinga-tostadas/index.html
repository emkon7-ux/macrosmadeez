Chicken Tinga Tostadas

Smoky chipotle shredded chicken piled on crispy baked corn tostadas.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, chipotle and garlic.

  2. Simmer the sauce with the onion, then add the chicken.

  3. Spread the beans on the tostadas.

  4. Top with the tinga, lettuce and a little crema.

TipUse half a chipotle for mild, or two if you like heat.
