Hungarian goulash soup.

    1Carrot
  • 4
    1 tbspPaprika

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with a little diced onion for 6 minutes.

  2. Stir in the paprika, then add the cubed potato, carrot and 4 cups water.

  3. Simmer 45 minutes until tender.

  4. Season and serve.

TipThe real goulash is a soup.
