Crispy Cod Tacos with Slaw

Fish taco night without the fryer. Baked cod, crunchy slaw and a tangy yogurt crema.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerMexico

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozCod fillet
  • 2Corn tortillas
  • 1 cupCabbage slaw
  • ¼ cupYogurt-lime crema

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season the cod and bake it at 425 for twelve minutes.

  2. Warm up the tortillas.

  3. Stir lime juice into the yogurt.

  4. Then flake in the cod and top with slaw and crema.

Tipa little panko on the cod makes it extra crispy.
