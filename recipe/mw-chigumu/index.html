Chigumu Cornbread

Baked maize and banana bread.

1 serving

    1/2 cupMaize flour
  • 2
    1Ripe banana
  • 3
    1Egg
  • 4
    1/4 cupLow-fat (1%) milk
  • 5
    1 tspBaking powder

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mash the banana and mix with the egg, milk, maize flour and baking powder.

  2. Pour into a small lined loaf tin.

  3. Bake at 350F for 35 minutes until set.
