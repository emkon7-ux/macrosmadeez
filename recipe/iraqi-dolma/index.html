Iraqi Dolma

Stuffed veggies and vine leaves with lean meat.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the beef, rice, diced onion and salt.

  2. Hollow the pepper and stuff it; roll the rest in the vine leaves.

  3. Pack in a pot with water to cover and a squeeze of lemon.

  4. Simmer covered 45 minutes.
