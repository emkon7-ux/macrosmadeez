Matoke with Beef

Steamed green bananas in a tomato beef stew.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerUganda40 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    3Green bananas (matoke), peeled
  • 2
    5 ozLean stewing beef (top or bottom round), cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3
    1Tomato
  • 4
    1Onion
  • 5
    1 tspCurry powder

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with onion and curry powder.

  2. Add the tomato and peeled bananas with 2 cups water.

  3. Cover and simmer 25 minutes until the bananas are soft, then mash lightly.
