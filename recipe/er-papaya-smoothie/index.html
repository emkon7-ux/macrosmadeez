Why it's good for you

  • Papaya's enzyme papain supports digestion.
  • One cup covers more than a day's vitamin C.
  • Low in calories but filling thanks to the protein.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Peel and seed the papaya and cut it into chunks.

  2. Blend with the Greek yogurt, lime juice and ice until smooth.

  3. Serve right away in a chilled glass.

TipUse very ripe papaya.
