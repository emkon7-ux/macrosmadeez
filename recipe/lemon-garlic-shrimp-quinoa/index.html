Lemon Garlic Shrimp & Quinoa

Lemony garlic shrimp over fluffy quinoa. Light, fresh and filling.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinner
400Calories
Protein
38g
Carbs
35g
Fat
10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozShrimp
  • ½ cupCooked quinoa
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 1Lemon

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the quinoa.

  2. Sear the shrimp with the garlic.

  3. Squeeze in the lemon.

  4. Then spoon it all over the quinoa.

Tipshrimp cook in about 2 minutes per side. Pull them when they curl into a C.
