Hamli with Chickpeas

Garlicky collard greens with chickpeas.

  • 3
    3 clovesGarlic

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Saute the sliced garlic in a little oil until golden.

  2. Add the chopped collards, chickpeas and a splash of water.

  3. Cover and cook 10 minutes until the greens are tender, then season with salt.
