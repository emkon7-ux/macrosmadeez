Fresh Shrimp Ceviche

Fresh, zesty and no stove needed. Eat it with cucumber slices or a few tortilla chips.

High proteinGluten-freeSnackPeru
220Calories

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozCooked shrimp, chopped
  • ½ cupLime juice
  • ½Cucumber, diced
  • ¼Red onion, diced
  • 1 handfulCilantro
  • 1Jalapeño

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Chop everything up small.

  2. Add the shrimp.

  3. Pour the lime juice over the top.

  4. Then chill it for fifteen minutes.

Tipstarting with cooked shrimp makes it quick and food-safe.
