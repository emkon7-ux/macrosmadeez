Cottage Cheese Berry Breakfast Bowl

The fastest high-protein breakfast there is. Sweet, creamy and crunchy.

High proteinVegetarianGluten-freeBreakfast

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 cupLow-fat cottage cheese
  • ½ cupMixed berries
  • 1 tbspWalnuts
  • 1 tspHoney

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Scoop the cottage cheese into a bowl.

  2. Top it with the berries.

  3. Sprinkle on the walnuts.

  4. Then finish with a drizzle of honey.

Tipblend the cottage cheese first if you don't love the texture.
