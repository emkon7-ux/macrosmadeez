Peanut Chicken Salad (Salata Dakwa)

A tomato-cucumber salad with a creamy peanut-lime dressing, topped with grilled chicken.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the peanut butter, lime and warm water into a dressing.

  2. Chop the tomato, cucumber and chili.

  3. Toss with the dressing.

  4. Top with sliced chicken.

TipPeanut butter powder keeps it lighter.
