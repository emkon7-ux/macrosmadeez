Shish Tawook

Garlicky Lebanese chicken skewers with a light toum-style yogurt sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the yogurt, garlic, lemon, paprika and tomato paste.

  2. Marinate the chicken for at least 1 hour.

  3. Skewer it and grill until golden.

  4. Serve with pickles, veggies and garlic yogurt.

TipGrate 1 garlic clove into extra yogurt for a quick lighter toum.
