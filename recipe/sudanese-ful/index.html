Sudanese Ful with Feta

Fava beans mashed with peanut oil, cumin and crumbled cheese.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Warm and roughly mash the beans.

  2. Season with cumin, salt and a little oil.

  3. Top with tomato, onion and chili.

  4. Crumble the feta over.

TipServe with a boiled egg for an extra protein kick.
