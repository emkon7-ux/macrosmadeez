Zoom-Koom Smoothie

Millet, ginger and tamarind drink blended into a smoothie.

Why it's good for you

  • Ginger helps digestion and soothes the stomach.
  • Millet gives slow-release carbs.
  • Dairy-free and refreshing in heat.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Soak the millet flour in a cup of water for 10 minutes.

  2. Blend with the ginger, tamarind and banana until smooth.

  3. Strain if you like and serve over ice.
