Firfir with Eggs

Torn injera tossed in berbere sauce with eggs.

  • 3

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the berbere with a little oil, onion and water for 3 minutes.

  2. Tear the injera and toss it in the sauce.

  3. Fry or scramble the eggs.

  4. Serve the eggs on top.

TipTeff injera is gluten-free.
