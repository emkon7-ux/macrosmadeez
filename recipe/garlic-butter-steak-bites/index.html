Garlic Butter Steak Bites

Juicy garlic butter steak bites with crisp green beans. 42g of protein.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinner
420Calories
Protein
42g
Carbs
10g
Fat
22g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozSirloin, cubed
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 1 tspButter
  • 1 cupGreen beans

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cut the steak into bites.

  2. Sear them in a really hot pan.

  3. Add the garlic and butter for the last minute.

  4. Then serve with green beans.

Tipdon't crowd the pan or the steak won't get that crust.
