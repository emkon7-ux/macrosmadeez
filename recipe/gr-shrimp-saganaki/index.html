Shrimp Saganaki

Shrimp in tomato and feta.

  • 4

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Simmer the tomatoes with garlic and oregano for 10 minutes.

  2. Nestle in the shrimp and crumble the feta over.

  3. Bake at 400F for 10 minutes until bubbling.
