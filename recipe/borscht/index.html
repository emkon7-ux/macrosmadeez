Beef Borscht

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the beef in 4 cups salted water for 20 minutes.

  2. Add the grated beets, shredded cabbage, carrot and cubed potato.

  3. Cook 30 minutes until tender.

  4. Serve with yogurt and dill.
