Ifisashi

Greens in a creamy peanut sauce.

    1Tomato
  • 4
    1Onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the diced onion and tomato in a pot over medium heat for 6 minutes.

  2. Add the sliced greens and 1/2 cup water and cook 8 minutes.

  3. Stir in the peanut butter until creamy.

  4. Simmer 10 minutes on low, season with salt and serve.
