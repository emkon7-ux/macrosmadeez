Baked Bean Fritters

Black-eyed pea fritters, baked instead of fried.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F.

  2. Blend the soaked peas with the onion, chili and salt.

  3. Spoon onto a lined, oiled tray.

  4. Bake 20 minutes, flipping once.

TipGreat with pepper sauce.
