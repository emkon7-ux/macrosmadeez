Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the cottage cheese, egg, flour and a pinch of salt.

  2. Shape into small patties.

  3. Cook in a lightly oiled pan on medium-low 3 to 4 minutes per side.

  4. Serve with yogurt or berries.
