Saffron Chicken Plov

Azerbaijani saffron rice with chicken and dried fruit.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2
    1/3 cupBasmati rice
  • 3
    1 pinchSaffron
  • 4
    1 tbspDried apricots & chestnuts

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Boil the rice in salted water 6 minutes, then drain.

  2. Cook the cubed chicken with the apricots and chestnuts for 8 minutes.

  3. Layer rice over the chicken, drizzle with saffron water, cover and steam on low 20 minutes.

  4. Serve.

TipSaffron gives a golden color.
