Shopska Salad with Chicken

Bulgaria's famous salad with grilled chicken.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season and grill the chicken 6 to 7 minutes per side; slice.

  2. Chop the tomato, cucumber and pepper and toss with a pinch of salt.

  3. Grate the cheese over.

  4. Top with the chicken.

TipGrated cheese is the signature.
