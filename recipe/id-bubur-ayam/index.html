Bubur Ayam

Rice porridge with chicken and crispy shallots.

    1 tspCrispy shallots
  • 4
    1Egg
  • 5

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Simmer the rice in plenty of water until it breaks down.

  2. Poach and shred the chicken.

  3. Top the porridge with chicken, egg, soy and crispy shallots.
