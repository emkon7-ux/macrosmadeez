Chocolate Protein Brownie Bites

Fudgy protein brownie bites. 3 bites = 220 cal and 20g protein.

VegetarianDessert
220Calories
Protein
20g
Carbs
20g
Fat
6g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • ½ cupOat flour
  • 1 scoopChocolate whey
  • 3 tbspCocoa powder
  • ½ cupGreek yogurt
  • 1Egg

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix everything into a thick batter.

  2. Spoon it into a mini muffin tin.

  3. Bake at 350 for twelve minutes.

  4. Let them cool so they get fudgy.

Tipslightly underbake them for the fudgiest middle.
