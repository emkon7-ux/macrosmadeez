Panamanian Sancocho

Chicken soup with culantro and yuca.

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast (cooked and shredded)
  • 2
    4 ozYuca or ñame
  • 3
    1Culantro
  • 4
    1Onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chicken and diced onion in 3 cups salted water for 15 minutes.

  2. Add the peeled, cubed yuca or ñame.

  3. Cook 20 minutes until tender, then shred the chicken back in.

  4. Stir in chopped culantro and serve.
