Beef Suya Skewers

Street-style spicy peanut-crusted beef skewers with onion and tomato.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozSirloin & thin strips
  • 2
    1 1/2 tbspSuya spice (yaji)
  • 3
    1Red onion, sliced
  • 4
    1Tomato, sliced
  • 5
    1 tspVegetable or canola oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toss the beef with oil and most of the suya spice.

  2. Thread onto skewers.

  3. Grill hot for 3 to 4 minutes per side.

  4. Dust with more spice and serve with the onion and tomato.

TipSuya spice has ground peanuts, so watch out for allergies.
