Riesling Chicken

Chicken braised in Riesling with mushrooms.

  • 3
    1 cupMushrooms
  • 4
    2 tbspPlain nonfat Greek yogurt

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the seasoned chicken in a pan 4 minutes per side.

  2. Add the sliced mushrooms and wine.

  3. Simmer 15 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.

  4. Take off the heat and stir in the yogurt.
