Mediterranean Chickpea Tuna Salad

A bright, no-cook Mediterranean salad with 38g of protein. Great for meal prep.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerGreece

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 canTuna, drained
  • ½ cupChickpeas
  • ½Cucumber
  • ½ cupCherry tomatoes
  • 1 tspOlive oil + lemon

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Chop the cucumber and tomatoes.

  2. Add the tuna and chickpeas.

  3. Dress it with olive oil and lemon.

  4. Then toss and dig in.

Tipit tastes even better after an hour in the fridge.
