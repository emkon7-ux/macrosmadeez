Beef Japchae

Glass noodles with beef, spinach and vegetables.

1 serving

  • 1
    2 ozSweet potato noodles
  • 2
    4 ozBeef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 3
    2 cupsSpinach & mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, bell pepper)
  • 4
    1 tbspTamari & sesame oil

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Cook the glass noodles, rinse and cut them shorter.

  2. Stir-fry the beef, then the vegetables, and blanch the spinach.

  3. Toss everything with tamari, sesame oil and sesame seeds.
