Why it's good for you

  • Black sesame is rich in calcium, iron and healthy fats.
  • Soy milk gives complete plant protein.
  • Dairy-free and naturally sweet.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add the black sesame paste, soy milk and banana to a blender.

  2. Blend with ice until smooth.

  3. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.
