Githeri

Maize and beans stewed with tomato and onion.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the diced onion and tomato in a little oil for 6 minutes.

  2. Add the corn, beans, cubed potato and 1 cup water.

  3. Simmer 20 minutes until the potato is soft.

  4. Season and serve.
