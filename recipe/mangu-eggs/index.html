Mangú with Eggs

Mashed plantains with pickled onions and eggs.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Boil the peeled plantain 15 minutes, then mash with a splash of the cooking water and salt.

  2. Quick-pickle the sliced red onion in a little vinegar for 10 minutes.

  3. Fry or scramble the eggs.

  4. Serve with the mangú, onions and cheese.
