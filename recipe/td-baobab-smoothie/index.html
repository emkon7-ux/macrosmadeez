Why it's good for you

  • Baobab is one of the richest fruit sources of vitamin C.
  • Its soluble fiber feeds good gut bacteria.
  • Yogurt and milk add 20g of protein.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add everything to the blender.

  2. Blend with ice until smooth.

  3. Taste and serve.
