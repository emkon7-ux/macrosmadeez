Za'atar Manakish

Flatbread with za'atar and labneh.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F.

  2. Mix the za'atar with a little olive oil and spread on the flatbread.

  3. Bake 8 minutes.

  4. Top with labneh.
