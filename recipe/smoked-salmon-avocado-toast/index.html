Smoked Salmon Avocado Toast

Avocado toast, upgraded. Smoked salmon and an egg bring it to 28g of protein.

Breakfast

Carbs

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 sliceSourdough
  • ¼Avocado
  • 3 ozSmoked salmon
  • 1Egg, soft boiled

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toast the sourdough.

  2. Smash the avocado on top.

  3. Layer on the salmon.

  4. Then add the egg and a pinch of seasoning.

Tipa 7-minute egg gives you that jammy yolk.
