Strawberry Protein Crepes

Light, thin crepes with a creamy Greek yogurt filling. 30g of protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 2Eggs
  • ¼ cupOat flour
  • ½ cupGreek yogurt
  • 1 cupStrawberries

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the eggs and oat flour with a splash of milk.

  2. Pour a thin layer in a hot pan and flip once.

  3. Spread on the yogurt.

  4. Then add strawberries and fold.

Tipswirl the pan right away so the crepe spreads thin.
