Tavče Gravče

Baked white beans with peppers and paprika.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Simmer the beans in 1 cup water for 10 minutes.

  2. Fry the diced onion with the paprika, mint and crumbled dried pepper for 5 minutes.

  3. Combine with the beans in a baking dish and season.

  4. Bake 30 minutes until bubbling.
