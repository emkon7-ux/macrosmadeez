Swabian Potato Salad

Vinegar potato salad.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    2Waxy potatoes
  • 2
    1 tbspVinegar
  • 3
    1Onion, finely diced
  • 4
    1/4 cupWarm broth
  • 5
    1 tspMustard

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel and slice while warm.

  2. Pour over warm broth mixed with vinegar, mustard and onion.

  3. Let it soak for 20 minutes before serving.
