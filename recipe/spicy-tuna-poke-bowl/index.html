Spicy Tuna Poke Bowl

A fresh poke bowl with a creamy, spicy sriracha yogurt drizzle. 40g of protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 4 ozSushi-grade tuna
  • ½ cupBrown rice
  • ½Cucumber
  • ¼ cupEdamame
  • 1 tbspSriracha yogurt

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cube the tuna.

  2. Add the rice to a bowl.

  3. Top with cucumber and edamame.

  4. Then drizzle on the spicy sauce.

Tipmix sriracha into Greek yogurt for a lighter spicy mayo.
