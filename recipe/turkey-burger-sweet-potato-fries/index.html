Turkey Burger & Sweet Potato Fries

A juicy turkey burger with crispy baked sweet potato fries. 40g of protein.

1 serving

  • 5 oz93% lean ground turkey
  • 1Whole wheat bun
  • 1 smallSweet potato
  • 1 tspSmoked paprika

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cut the sweet potato into wedges.

  2. Season them and bake at 425 for twenty five minutes.

  3. Form the patty and cook it through.

  4. Then build your burger.

Tippress a dent in the middle of the patty so it stays flat.
