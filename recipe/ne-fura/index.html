Fura Breakfast Bowl

Millet balls mashed into fermented milk.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    1/3 cupMillet flour
  • 2
    3/4 cupKefir
  • 3
    1 sliceGinger
  • 4
    1 tspHoney

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mix the millet flour with a little water and grated ginger, roll into balls and steam 15 minutes.

  2. Mash the warm millet into the cold kefir.

  3. Sweeten with honey and eat with a spoon.

TipFura da nono style.
