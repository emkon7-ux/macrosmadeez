Sadza ne Nyama

Maize sadza with beef stew.

1 serving

    1/2 cupMaize meal

    6 ozLean stewing beef (top or bottom round), cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 3
    1Tomato
  • 4
    1Onion

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with onion, add the tomato and simmer 40 minutes.

  2. Stir the maize meal into boiling water until stiff to make sadza.

  3. Serve the sadza with the stew.
