Lablabi

Chickpea soup with cumin, harissa and torn bread.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chickpeas in broth with garlic.

  2. Tear the bread into a bowl.

  3. Ladle the soup over and top with harissa and cumin.
