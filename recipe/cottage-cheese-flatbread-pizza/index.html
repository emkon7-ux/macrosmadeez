Cottage Cheese Flatbread Pizza

Pizza night with a 2-ingredient crust. Cottage cheese and eggs bake into a sturdy flatbread.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerItaly

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • ½ cupCottage cheese
  • 2Eggs
  • ¼ cupMarinara
  • ¼ cupPart-skim mozzarella
  • 10 slicesTurkey pepperoni

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese and eggs.

  2. Spread it thin and bake at 400 for twenty minutes.

  3. Add the sauce, cheese and pepperoni.

  4. Then bake till it's bubbly.

Tipuse parchment paper so the crust peels right off.
