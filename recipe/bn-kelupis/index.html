Kelupis

Glutinous rice in leaves.

  • 3
    2Leaves for wrapping

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soak the sticky rice 2 hours, drain and mix with the coconut milk and a pinch of salt.

  2. Wrap tightly in the leaves or foil.

  3. Steam 40 minutes.

  4. Unwrap and serve.
