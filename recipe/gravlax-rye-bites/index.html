Gravlax Rye Bites

Dill-cured salmon on crisp rye with a mustard yogurt.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    3 ozGravlax or smoked salmon
  • 2
    2Rye crispbreads
  • 3
    2 tbspPlain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 4
    1 tspSweet mustard
  • 5
    1 sprigFresh dill
  • 6
    1Lemon wedge

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the yogurt and mustard.

  2. Spread it on the crispbreads.

  3. Fold the salmon on top.

  4. Finish with dill and a squeeze of lemon.

TipGreat for parties: break the crispbread into small squares.
