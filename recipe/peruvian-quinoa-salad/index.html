Peruvian Quinoa Salad

Andean quinoa with corn, beans, peppers and a zippy lime dressing.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cool the cooked quinoa on a tray.

  2. Toss with the beans, corn and peppers.

  3. Whisk the lime, oil, salt and a little ají.

  4. Dress, then fold in the cilantro.

TipIt keeps for 4 days in the fridge, so it's great for meal prep.
