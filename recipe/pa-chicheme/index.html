Why it's good for you

  • Corn adds fiber and slow carbs.
  • Milk and yogurt add calcium and protein.
  • Cinnamon adds flavor without sugar.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add the cooked corn, milk, yogurt and cinnamon to a blender.

  2. Blend until smooth.

  3. Strain if you like and serve cold.
