Baked Chicken Schnitzel

A thin, crispy oven-baked cutlet with a lemony cucumber salad.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Dip the chicken in egg, then panko.

  2. Bake on a rack at 425°F for 15 minutes.

  3. Toss the cucumbers with yogurt, dill and vinegar.

  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

TipBaking on a rack keeps the bottom crunchy.
