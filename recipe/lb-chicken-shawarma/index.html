Chicken Shawarma Plate

Spiced chicken with garlic toum and pickles.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Rub the chicken thighs with shawarma spice, lemon and garlic and rest 30 minutes.

  2. Roast at 425F for 25 minutes until charred at the edges, then slice thin.

  3. Serve over rice with a spoon of toum, pickles and parsley.

TipUse yogurt-based toum.
