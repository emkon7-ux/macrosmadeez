Why it's good for you

  • Papaya contains papain, an enzyme that helps digestion.
  • Mango and papaya are packed with vitamin C and vitamin A.
  • Yogurt adds protein so it works as a light breakfast.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Blend each fruit separately with a little yogurt.

  2. Layer in a tall glass.

  3. Finish with lime.
