Spiced Grilled Beef

Charcoal-grilled beef with a spicy peanut rub.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Rub the steak all over with the peanut chili rub and a pinch of salt.

  2. Grill or sear over high heat 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium.

  3. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain.

  4. Serve with sliced onion and tomato.

TipKilishi spice works great here.
