Lighter Macaroni Pie

A Bajan baked macaroni with less cheese.

    1 ozReduced-fat sharp cheddar
  • 3
    1/2 cupLow-fat cottage cheese
  • 4
    1Egg

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F and boil the macaroni until just tender. Drain.

  2. Mix with the grated cheddar, cottage cheese, beaten egg, salt and pepper.

  3. Spread in a small greased dish and bake 25 minutes until golden.

  4. Cool 5 minutes and slice.

TipCottage cheese adds protein.
