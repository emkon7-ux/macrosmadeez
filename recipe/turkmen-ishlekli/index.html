Lamb & Onion Kebab

Grilled lamb with onion and herbs.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Marinate the lamb with grated onion and salt for 30 minutes.

  2. Grill 8 to 10 minutes, turning.

  3. Serve with sliced onion.

  4. Add the herbs.
