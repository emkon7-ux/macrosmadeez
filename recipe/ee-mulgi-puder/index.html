Mulgipuder with Chicken

Potato-barley mash with chicken.

1 serving

  • 1
    4 ozPotato
  • 2
    1/4 cupBarley
  • 3
    5 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the barley in 1 cup salted water for 30 minutes.

  2. Boil the potato 15 minutes and mash with the barley.

  3. Season and pan-cook the chicken 6 to 7 minutes per side.

  4. Serve together.

TipBarley is not gluten-free; swap in rice for GF.
