Lao Beef Larb

Minced beef salad with herbs and toasted rice.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a dry pan for 5 minutes, breaking it up.

  2. Take off the heat and stir in the lime juice and fish sauce.

  3. Toss with the mint and sliced shallot or onion.

  4. Sprinkle with the toasted rice powder.

TipLarb is the national dish of Laos.
