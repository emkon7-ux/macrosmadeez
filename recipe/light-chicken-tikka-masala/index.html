Light Chicken Tikka Masala

A lighter tikka masala with a creamy yogurt sauce and cauliflower rice.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerIndia

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozChicken breast, cubed
  • ¼ cupGreek yogurt
  • ½ cupCrushed tomatoes
  • 2 tspTikka spices
  • 1 cupCauliflower rice

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toss the chicken in yogurt and spices.

  2. Sear it in a hot pan.

  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer ten minutes.

  4. Then serve it over cauliflower rice.

Tipeven 15 minutes in the yogurt marinade makes the chicken super tender.
