Coconut Protein Bread

Garifuna-style coconut bread.

1 serving

    1/3 cupAll-purpose flour
  • 2
    1/4 cupCoconut milk
  • 3
    1/2 scoopVanilla or unflavored whey protein powder

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.

  2. Mix the flour, protein powder, coconut milk and a pinch of baking powder into a soft dough.

  3. Shape into a small roll.

  4. Bake 20 minutes until golden.
