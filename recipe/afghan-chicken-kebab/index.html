Afghan Chicken Kebab

Yogurt-marinated chicken kebabs with chutney.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the yogurt, minced garlic, lemon juice and salt and marinate the cubed chicken 30 minutes.

  2. Skewer.

  3. Grill 10 to 12 minutes, turning.

  4. Serve with the green chutney.

TipCilantro chutney is a must.
