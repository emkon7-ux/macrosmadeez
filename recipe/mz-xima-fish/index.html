Xima & Grilled Fish

Maize xima with fish.

1 serving

  • 1
    1/2 cupMaize flour
  • 2
    6 ozSkinless hake fillets (or tilapia)
  • 3
    1Lemon
  • 4
    1 tspPeri-peri

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Rub the fish with peri-peri and lemon and grill until flaky.

  2. Stir the maize flour into boiling water until thick to make xima.

  3. Serve with lemon wedges.
