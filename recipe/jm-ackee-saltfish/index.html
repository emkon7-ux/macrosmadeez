Ackee & Saltfish

Jamaica's national breakfast.

    1Onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soak the saltfish overnight, boil 10 minutes and flake.

  2. Sauté the diced onion, tomato and a little scotch bonnet for 5 minutes.

  3. Add the fish.

  4. Gently fold in the drained ackee and heat 2 minutes.

TipUse canned ackee.
