Why it's good for you

  • Hibiscus is rich in antioxidants linked to healthy blood pressure.
  • Mango brings vitamin C and fiber.
  • Yogurt adds protein and probiotics.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Steep dried hibiscus in hot water for 10 minutes, then chill.

  2. Blend the karkadeh with the mango, yogurt and ice.

  3. Serve cold with a slice of lime.
