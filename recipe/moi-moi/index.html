Moi Moi

A steamed bean pudding made from peeled black-eyed peas and peppers.

VeganVegetarianGluten-freeSnackNigeria60 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    3/4 cupBlack-eyed peas, peeled
  • 2
    1/2Red bell pepper
  • 3
    1/2Onion
  • 4
    1/2Scotch bonnet
  • 5
    1 tspVegetable or canola oil
  • 6
    1/2 cupVeggie stock

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soak and peel the beans.

  2. Blend with the peppers, onion and stock until smooth.

  3. Season and pour into ramekins or foil cups.

  4. Steam for 45 minutes until set.

TipAdd flaked fish or egg for more protein if you're not vegan.
