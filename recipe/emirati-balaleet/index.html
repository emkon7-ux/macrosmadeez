Balaleet with Egg

Sweet saffron vermicelli topped with an omelet.

  • 3
    1 pinchSaffron & cardamom

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the vermicelli in water with the saffron, cardamom and a little sugar for 5 minutes; drain.

  2. Beat the eggs and cook into a thin omelet.

  3. Plate the vermicelli.

  4. Lay the omelet on top.
