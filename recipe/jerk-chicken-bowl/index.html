Jerk Chicken Bowl

Spicy, smoky jerk chicken with rice and peas and a mango salsa.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Rub the chicken with the jerk marinade and let it sit for 30 minutes.

  2. Grill or roast until charred and cooked through.

  3. Toss the mango with the onion, lime and cilantro.

  4. Serve over rice and peas with the salsa.

TipScotch bonnet is hot. Remove the seeds if you want a milder jerk.
