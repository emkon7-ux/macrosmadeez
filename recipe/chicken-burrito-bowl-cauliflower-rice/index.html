Chicken Burrito Bowl (Cauliflower Rice)

Swapping rice for cauliflower rice saves a ton of calories, and with chicken, beans and salsa piled on you honestly don't miss it. Great for meal prep too.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozGrilled chicken breast
  • 1 cupCauliflower rice
  • ½ cupBlack beans
  • ¼ cupSalsa
  • ½Lime

Steps 5 easy steps

  1. First, sauté the cauli rice till it's a little golden.

  2. Warm up the beans.

  3. Slice the chicken and pile it on.

  4. Spoon the salsa over the top.

  5. Then squeeze that lime over everything.

Tiplet the cauli rice sit in the hot pan for a minute before stirring so it gets golden instead of soggy.
