Lamb Souvlaki Wrap

Melbourne-style lamb wrap.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season the lamb with oregano, lemon and salt.

  2. Grill 8 minutes, turning.

  3. Warm the pita and fill with salad and lamb.

  4. Add the tzatziki and wrap.
