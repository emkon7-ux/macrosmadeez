Boiled beef with apple-horseradish and broth.

  • 2
    1Root veggies

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the beef with the root vegetables in salted water for 2 hours.

  2. Slice the beef.

  3. Serve in a bowl with some broth and the vegetables.

  4. Add the apple horseradish on the side.
