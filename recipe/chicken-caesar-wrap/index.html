Chicken Caesar Wrap

Your favorite salad, wrapped up and ready to go. 42g protein for 380 calories.

High proteinLunch & dinner

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozGrilled chicken
  • 1 cupRomaine, chopped
  • 1 tbspParmesan
  • 2 tbspGreek yogurt Caesar
  • 1Low-carb tortilla

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toss the romaine with the dressing.

  2. Add the chicken and parmesan.

  3. Pile it onto the tortilla.

  4. Then roll it up nice and tight.

Tipwarm the tortilla for a few seconds so it rolls without cracking.
