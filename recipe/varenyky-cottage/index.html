Cottage Cheese Varenyky

Dumplings filled with cottage cheese.

1 serving

  • 1
    1/2 cupLow-fat cottage cheese
  • 2
    10Dumpling wrappers
  • 3
    2 tbspPlain nonfat yogurt

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Put a spoonful of cottage cheese in each wrapper.

  2. Wet the edges and seal.

  3. Boil in salted water 5 minutes until they float.

  4. Serve with yogurt.
