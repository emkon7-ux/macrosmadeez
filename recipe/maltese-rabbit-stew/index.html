Stuffat tal-Fenek

Malta's rabbit stew with wine and tomato.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the meat in a little oil for 6 minutes.

  2. Add the garlic, bay, wine and tomato.

  3. Simmer covered 75 minutes until tender.

  4. Season and serve.
