Kabuli Pulao

Afghanistan's national rice with lamb, carrots and raisins.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the lamb.

  2. Add the spices and water and simmer.

  3. Cook the rice in the broth.

  4. Top with carrots and raisins.
