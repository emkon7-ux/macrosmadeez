Lean Beef Zigni

A spicy berbere beef stew, the Eritrean way.

    2Red onions, minced

  • 5

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the onions slowly until soft.

  2. Add the berbere, garlic and tomato paste.

  3. Add the beef and water and simmer for 35 minutes.

  4. Serve with teff injera.

TipLet it rest for 10 minutes. It thickens as it cools.
