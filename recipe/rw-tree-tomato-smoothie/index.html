Why it's good for you

  • Tamarillo is rich in vitamins A, C and E.
  • Low sugar compared with most fruit smoothies.
  • Protein from yogurt helps keep you full.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Halve the tamarillos and scoop out the flesh.

  2. Blend with banana, Greek yogurt and ice.

  3. Strain out the seeds and serve cold.
