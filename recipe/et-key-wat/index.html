Lean Beef Key Wat

Ethiopia's rich berbere beef stew, made with lean beef.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Cook the onions down slowly until jammy.

  2. Add berbere and kibbeh, then the beef.

  3. Simmer covered 30 minutes until tender.

TipServe with injera or teff.
