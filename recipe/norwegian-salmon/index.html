Norwegian Salmon & Dill

Pan-seared salmon with dill potatoes.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season the salmon and sear skin-side down 4 minutes, then 2 minutes on the other side.

  2. Boil the potatoes 15 minutes.

  3. Toss the potatoes with chopped dill and salt.

  4. Serve with lemon.

TipCrispy skin is the goal.
