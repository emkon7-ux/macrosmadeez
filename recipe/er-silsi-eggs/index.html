Silsi with Eggs

Berbere tomato sauce with eggs.

  • 5

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Cook the chopped onion in oil until soft, then add the tomatoes and berbere.

  2. Simmer 8 minutes into a thick spicy sauce.

  3. Stir in the beaten eggs and cook gently until just set.

TipWith taita bread.
