Date-filled cardamom cookies.

    3Dates
  • 3
    1 tspButter
  • 4

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Rub the butter into the flour and cardamom with a splash of water to make a dough.

  2. Roll out, spread with mashed dates and roll up.

  3. Slice and bake at 375F for 15 minutes.
