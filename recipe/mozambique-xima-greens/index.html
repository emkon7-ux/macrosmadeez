Matapa with Beans

Cassava leaves in coconut-peanut with beans.

    1/3 cupCoconut milk

    1 tbspPeanuts

    1/2 cupPinto or sugar beans (canned, rinsed)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the finely chopped greens in 1/2 cup water for 15 minutes.

  2. Stir in the coconut milk and ground peanuts.

  3. Add the beans.

  4. Simmer 10 minutes and season.
