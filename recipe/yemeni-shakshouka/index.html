Yemeni Shakshouka

Eggs in a spicy tomato sauce.

1 serving

  • 1
    2Eggs
  • 2
    1/2 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs
  • 3
    1 cupTomato
  • 4
    1 tspZhug

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the tomato with a pinch of salt for 8 minutes.

  2. Make wells and add the eggs and whites.

  3. Cover and cook 5 minutes until set.

  4. Top with the zhug.

TipZhug is a cilantro chili sauce.
