Rice Bread

Sweet banana rice-flour bread.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Mash the banana.

  2. Mix with the rice flour, egg and a pinch of baking powder.

  3. Pour into a small greased dish.

  4. Bake 35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
