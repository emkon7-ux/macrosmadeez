Moroho & Chicken

Spinach-cabbage greens with chicken.

    3 cupsSpinach & cabbage

    1Onion
  • 4
    1Tomato

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season the cubed chicken and cook in a pan for 8 minutes until done. Set aside.

  2. In the same pan, cook the diced onion and tomato for 4 minutes.

  3. Add the shredded spinach and cabbage and sauté 6 minutes until tender.

  4. Return the chicken, season with salt and pepper and serve.
