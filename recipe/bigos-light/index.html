Lighter Bigos

Hunter's stew with sauerkraut and lean meats.

    2 ozTurkey kielbasa
  • 3
    1 cupSauerkraut
  • 4
    1 cupCabbage

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the pork and sliced kielbasa for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the sauerkraut, shredded cabbage and 1 cup water.

  3. Simmer covered 75 minutes.

  4. Season with pepper.
