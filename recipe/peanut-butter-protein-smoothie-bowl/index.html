Peanut Butter Protein Smoothie Bowl

A thick, spoonable smoothie bowl that tastes like dessert and packs 35g of protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1Frozen banana
  • 1 scoopChocolate whey
  • 2 tbspPeanut butter powder
  • ½ cupAlmond milk
  • 2 tbspGranola

Why it's good for you

  • High protein to keep you full for hours and support muscle recovery.
  • Peanut butter adds healthy fats that slow digestion and steady your energy.
  • Frozen banana gives a thick, ice-cream texture plus potassium.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the banana, whey, peanut butter powder and milk.

  2. Add milk a little at a time so it stays thick.

  3. Spoon it into a bowl.

  4. Then top with granola and banana.

Tipfrozen banana is the secret to that ice cream texture.
