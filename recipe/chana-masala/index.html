Chana Masala

A cozy chickpea curry that's naturally vegan and packed with fiber.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the onion in the oil until golden.

  2. Add the garlic, ginger and spices and cook for 1 minute.

  3. Stir in the tomatoes and chickpeas.

  4. Simmer for 15 minutes, then finish with lemon and cilantro.

TipMash a few chickpeas against the pot to make it thicker.
