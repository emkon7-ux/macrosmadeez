Hausa Koko & Peanuts

Spiced millet porridge.

1 serving

  • 1
    1/3 cupMillet flour
  • 2
    1 tspGinger & cloves
  • 3
    1 tbspPeanuts

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the millet flour into 1 cup cold water.

  2. Add the ginger and cloves.

  3. Cook, stirring, 8 minutes until thick.

  4. Top with peanuts.
