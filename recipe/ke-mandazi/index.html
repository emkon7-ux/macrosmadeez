Baked Mandazi

Cardamom coconut doughnuts, baked.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    1/2 cupAll-purpose flour
  • 2
    2 tbspCoconut milk
  • 3
    1 tspSugar
  • 4
    1/2 tspBaking powder
  • 5
    1 pinchCardamom

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and cardamom, then add coconut milk to make a soft dough.

  2. Roll out and cut into triangles.

  3. Bake at 400F for 12 minutes until puffed and golden.

TipBest with chai.
