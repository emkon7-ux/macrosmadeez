Niçoise Salad

The bistro classic with tuna, egg, potatoes, green beans and olives.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Boil the potatoes and blanch the beans.

  2. Arrange the greens, potatoes, beans and tomatoes.

  3. Add the tuna, halved egg and olives.

  4. Drizzle with the vinaigrette.

TipDress the potatoes while they're warm so they soak it up.
