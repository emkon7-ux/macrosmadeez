Lighter Bryndzové Halušky

Potato dumplings with sheep cheese.

    1/4 cupAll-purpose flour
  • 3
    1 ozBryndza or feta

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Grate the potato and mix with the flour and a pinch of salt into a soft dough.

  2. Push small bits through a colander or off a board into boiling salted water.

  3. Cook 3 minutes until they float, then drain.

  4. Toss with the crumbled cheese.
