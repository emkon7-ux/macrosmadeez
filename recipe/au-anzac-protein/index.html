Protein ANZAC Bites

Oat and coconut bites.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    1/3 cupOats
  • 2
    1 tbspCoconut
  • 3
    1/2 scoopVanilla or unflavored whey protein powder
  • 4
    1 tspGolden syrup

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.

  2. Mix the oats, coconut, protein powder, golden syrup and a splash of water.

  3. Shape into small balls and flatten on a lined tray.

  4. Bake 12 minutes.
