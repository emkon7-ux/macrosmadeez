Turkey Taco Lettuce Cups

Taco night, lightened up. Crunchy lettuce shells and Greek yogurt sour cream keep it at 320 calories.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerMexico

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 oz99% lean ground turkey
  • 1 tbspTaco seasoning
  • ¼ cupPico de gallo
  • ¼ cupGreek yogurt “sour cream”
  • 4 leavesButter lettuce

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. First, brown the turkey.

  2. Stir in the taco seasoning with a splash of water.

  3. Spoon it into the lettuce cups.

  4. Then top with pico and a dollop of yogurt.

Tipa splash of water with the seasoning makes it saucy instead of dry.
