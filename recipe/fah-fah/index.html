Fah-Fah (Goat Soup)

A spicy goat and vegetable soup, Djibouti's national dish.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the meat with spices for 40 minutes.

  2. Add the veggies and chili.

  3. Cook for 15 minutes more.

  4. Finish with cilantro.

TipChicken thighs work if you can't find goat.
