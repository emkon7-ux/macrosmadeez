Sopa de Maní

A creamy peanut soup with chicken.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chicken in 3 cups salted water for 15 minutes, then remove and shred.

  2. Whisk the ground peanuts into the broth.

  3. Add the diced potato, carrot and peas and cook 15 minutes.

  4. Return the chicken and simmer until creamy.
