Tunisian Beef Mloukhia

Dark, slow-cooked jute stew with beef.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    7 ozLean stewing beef (top or bottom round), cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2
    2 tbspMloukhia powder
  • 3
    1 tbspOlive oil
  • 4
    2Bay leaves

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toast the mloukhia powder in the olive oil for 2 minutes, stirring.

  2. Add the beef, bay leaves and 3 cups water.

  3. Simmer on low 45 minutes, stirring often, until thick and dark.

  4. Season and serve.

TipMade at New Year.
