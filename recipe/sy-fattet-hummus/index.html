Fattet Hummus

Chickpeas, toasted bread and yogurt.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Break the toasted pita into a bowl.

  2. Spoon over the warm chickpeas with a little of their liquid.

  3. Top with yogurt mixed with tahini and garlic, then the toasted pine nuts.
