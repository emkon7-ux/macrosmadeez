Baked Falafel Bowl

Oven-baked falafel with salad and tahini.

  • 4

  • 5

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the chickpeas, herbs and spices.

  2. Shape into balls.

  3. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes.

  4. Serve on the salad with tahini.

TipUse soaked raw chickpeas, not canned.
