Moambe Chicken (Lighter)

The Congolese national dish: chicken in a rich palm nut sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the chicken.

  2. Add the onion, garlic and tomatoes.

  3. Stir in the palm nut cream and water.

  4. Simmer for 30 minutes.

TipPeanut butter is a common substitute for palm nut cream.
