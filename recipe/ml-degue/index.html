Millet couscous with yogurt and nutmeg.

  • 2
    3/4 cupPlain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Pour boiling water over the millet couscous, cover 5 minutes and fluff.

  2. Cool, then stir into the yogurt.

  3. Sweeten lightly if you like.

  4. Dust with nutmeg.
