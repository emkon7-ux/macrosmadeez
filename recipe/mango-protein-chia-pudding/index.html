Mango Protein Chia Pudding

Creamy chia pudding with 28g of protein and a tropical mango topping.

VegetarianGluten-freeBreakfast
300Calories
Protein
28g
Carbs
25g
Fat
10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 3 tbspChia seeds
  • 1 cupAlmond milk
  • 1 scoopVanilla protein
  • ½ cupMango

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the chia, milk and protein together.

  2. Let it sit five minutes, then whisk again.

  3. Chill it overnight.

  4. Then top with mango.

Tipthe second whisk stops the chia from clumping.
