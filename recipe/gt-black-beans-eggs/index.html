Huevos with Black Beans

Eggs, black beans, plantain and salsa.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Bake the sliced plantain at 425°F for 15 minutes, flipping once.

  2. Warm the black beans in a small pan with a pinch of salt, mashing a little.

  3. Fry or scramble the eggs in a non-stick pan.

  4. Plate the eggs with the beans, plantain and salsa.
