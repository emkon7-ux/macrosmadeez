Pondu with Smoked Fish

Cassava leaves stewed with smoked fish, onion and garlic.

    4 ozSmoked mackerel

  • 5

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the greens with onion and garlic.

  2. Add the pepper and cook for 20 minutes.

  3. Flake in the smoked fish.

  4. Simmer 10 more minutes.

TipFrozen cassava leaves are available in African grocers.
