Spinach Pkhali

Spinach and walnut balls with garlic and herbs.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    3 cupsSpinach
  • 2
    2 tbspWalnuts
  • 3
    1 cloveGarlic
  • 4
    1 tbspCilantro

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Blanch the spinach and squeeze it dry.

  2. Blend walnuts, garlic and herbs into a paste, then mix with spinach.

  3. Shape into balls and press a pomegranate seed on each.
