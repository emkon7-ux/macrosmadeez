Protein Bircher Muesli

The original overnight oats, made in Switzerland.

  • 3

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix the oats and yogurt with a splash of milk.

  2. Grate in the apple.

  3. Refrigerate overnight.

  4. Top with chopped hazelnuts.
