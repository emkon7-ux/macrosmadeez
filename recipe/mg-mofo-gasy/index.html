Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Whisk the rice flour, coconut milk, sugar and a pinch of baking powder.

  2. Rest 10 minutes.

  3. Cook spoonfuls in a lightly oiled pan 2 minutes per side.

  4. Serve warm.
