Steamed Ginger-Scallion Fish

A Cantonese classic: flaky white fish, sizzling ginger oil and soy.

High proteinLunch & dinnerChina20 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozCod or sea bass fillet
  • 2
    1 tbspGinger, julienned
  • 3
    3Scallions, julienned
  • 4
    1 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
  • 5
    2 tspNeutral oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Steam the fish over ginger slices for 8 to 10 minutes.

  2. Pile the scallions and ginger on top.

  3. Heat the oil until smoking and pour it over to sizzle.

  4. Drizzle with soy sauce and serve with greens.

TipIt's done when the fish flakes easily with a fork.
