Fish Liboke

Fish steamed in leaves with tomato and chili.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and season the fish.

  2. Put it on foil with the sliced tomato, onion and a little chili.

  3. Wrap tightly and bake 25 minutes.

  4. Serve.

TipBanana leaves are traditional.
