Togolese Peanut Stew

Chicken in a peanut-tomato sauce.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2
    1 tbspPeanut butter
  • 3
    1/2 cupTomato
  • 4
    1Onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cube the chicken, season, and brown in a pot over medium-high heat for 5 minutes.

  2. Add the diced onion and cook 3 minutes, then the tomato and cook 5 minutes.

  3. Stir in the peanut butter with 3/4 cup water until smooth.

  4. Simmer 20 minutes until thick and the chicken is tender. Season with salt.
