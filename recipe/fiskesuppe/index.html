Fiskesuppe (Fish Soup)

Creamy fish soup with root veggies.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the sliced carrot and leek in 1 1/2 cups stock for 10 minutes.

  2. Stir in the milk.

  3. Add the fish in chunks.

  4. Cook gently 5 minutes and season.
