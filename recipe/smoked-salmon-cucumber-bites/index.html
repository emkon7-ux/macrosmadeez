Smoked Salmon Cucumber Bites

A no-cook snack that looks fancy. Mixing Greek yogurt into the cream cheese lightens it up.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1Cucumber, in rounds
  • 2 tbspLight cream cheese
  • 2 tbspGreek yogurt
  • 3 ozSmoked salmon
  • 1 sprigFresh dill

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Slice the cucumber into thick rounds.

  2. Mix the cream cheese and yogurt.

  3. Spread a little on each one and add the salmon.

  4. Then finish with a bit of dill.

Tippat the cucumber rounds dry so the spread sticks.
