Shrimp Cauliflower Fried “Rice”

Fried rice cravings, handled. Cauliflower rice keeps it light, shrimp and egg whites bring the protein.

High proteinLunch & dinnerChina

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 2 cupsCauliflower rice
  • 5 ozShrimp
  • ½ cupEgg whites
  • ½ cupPeas
  • 1 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the shrimp, then set them aside.

  2. Scramble the egg whites.

  3. Add the cauli rice and peas and fry it up.

  4. Then toss it all with soy sauce.

Tipuse a really hot pan so the cauli rice fries instead of steams.
