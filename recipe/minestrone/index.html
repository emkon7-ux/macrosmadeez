Minestrone

A big pot of veggie and bean soup with a little pasta.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Sweat the onion, carrot and celery in a little oil.

  2. Add the tomatoes, broth and zucchini and simmer for 15 minutes.

  3. Add the pasta and beans and cook until tender.

  4. Stir in the spinach and top with basil.

TipUse gluten-free pasta and it's fully gluten-free.
