Apple Cinnamon Protein Oatmeal

Whisking egg whites into oatmeal makes it fluffy and adds protein you can't taste.

VegetarianBreakfast
320Calories
Protein
28g
Carbs
45g
Fat
4g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • ½ cupRolled oats
  • ½ cupEgg whites
  • ½Apple, diced
  • 1 tspCinnamon

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the oats with water.

  2. Whisk in the egg whites till it's fluffy.

  3. Stir in the apple.

  4. Then top it with cinnamon.

Tipkeep stirring when the whites go in so they don't scramble.
