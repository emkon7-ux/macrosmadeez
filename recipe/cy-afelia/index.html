Pork Afelia

Pork braised with red wine and coriander seed.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Marinate the pork in red wine and crushed coriander seed overnight.

  2. Brown it, then braise in the marinade for 45 minutes.

  3. Serve with bulgur and yogurt.
