Turkey Sweet Potato Breakfast Hash

A hearty one-pan breakfast hash. Make a big batch for the week.

High proteinGluten-freeBreakfast
380Calories
Protein
40g
Carbs
30g
Fat
10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 4 ozLean ground turkey
  • 1 smallSweet potato, cubed
  • ½Bell pepper
  • ½ cupEgg whites

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Crisp the sweet potato in a hot pan.

  2. Add the turkey and brown it.

  3. Toss in the pepper.

  4. Then scramble in the egg whites.

Tipmicrowave the sweet potato for 2 minutes first so it crisps faster.
