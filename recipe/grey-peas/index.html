Grey Peas with Bacon

Latvian grey peas with turkey bacon and onion.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the soaked peas for 40 minutes until soft (or warm canned chickpeas).

  2. Fry the chopped turkey bacon and onion for 6 minutes.

  3. Stir into the peas.

  4. Season with salt and pepper.

TipChickpeas work if you can't find grey peas.
