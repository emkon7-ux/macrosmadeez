Bún Chả

Grilled pork patties with rice noodles and herbs.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mix the pork with garlic, fish sauce and pepper and shape small patties.

  2. Grill until caramelized while the noodles cook.

  3. Serve the patties in nuoc cham with noodles and herbs on the side.
