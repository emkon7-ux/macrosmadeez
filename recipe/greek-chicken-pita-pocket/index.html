Greek Chicken Pita Pocket

A fresh, crunchy pita pocket with 40g of protein. Perfect packed lunch.

High proteinLunch & dinnerGreece

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1Whole wheat pita
  • 4 ozGrilled chicken
  • ½Cucumber
  • ½ cupCherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbspTzatziki

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Warm up the pita.

  2. Chop the cucumber and tomatoes.

  3. Stuff it with the chicken and veggies.

  4. Then spoon in the tzatziki.
