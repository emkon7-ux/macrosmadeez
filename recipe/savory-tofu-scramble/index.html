Savory Tofu Scramble

A plant-based breakfast that tastes like scrambled eggs. 28g of protein, no eggs needed.

VeganVegetarianGluten-freeBreakfast

Protein

Carbs
12g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 7 ozFirm tofu
  • ½ tspTurmeric
  • 2 tbspNutritional yeast
  • 1 cupSpinach
  • ½ cupCherry tomatoes

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Crumble the tofu into a hot pan.

  2. Stir in the turmeric and nutritional yeast.

  3. Toss in the spinach and tomatoes.

  4. Then cook till it's hot and a little golden.

Tippress the tofu for ten minutes first so it gets fluffy, not soggy.
