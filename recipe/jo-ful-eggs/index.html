Ful with Eggs

Fava beans with cumin, lemon and eggs.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Warm the fava beans and mash them roughly with garlic, cumin and lemon.

  2. Fry or boil the eggs.

  3. Spoon the ful into a bowl, drizzle with olive oil and top with the eggs.
