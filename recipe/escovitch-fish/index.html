Escovitch Fish

Pan-seared snapper topped with tangy pickled peppers and onions.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the vinegar with the allspice and veggies for 3 minutes.

  2. Season the fish and sear it until crisp.

  3. Spoon the pickled veggies and some liquid over the top.

  4. Let it sit for 5 minutes before eating.

TipIt's even better the next day, cold.
