Chapati & Egg Wrap

Kenyan rolex-style wrap with eggs and tomato.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Whisk the eggs with chopped tomato, cabbage and salt.

  2. Pour into a hot pan and lay the chapati on top while the egg sets.

  3. Flip, cook 30 seconds and roll up.
