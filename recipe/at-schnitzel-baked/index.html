Baked Chicken Wiener Schnitzel

A thin, crisp cutlet with potato salad.

1 serving

  • 1
    6 ozBoneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2
    1/3 cupPanko
  • 3
    4 ozPotato salad (vinegar)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and pound the chicken thin.

  2. Season, dip in a little beaten egg, then press into the panko.

  3. Bake 15 minutes, flipping once.

  4. Serve with the potato salad and lemon.

TipAustrian potato salad uses vinegar.
