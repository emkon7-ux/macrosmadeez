Baked Fry Jacks & Eggs

Belizean breakfast with beans and eggs.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Roll the dough thin and cut into pieces.

  2. Bake 10 minutes until puffed.

  3. Fry or scramble the eggs.

  4. Serve with the warm beans.
