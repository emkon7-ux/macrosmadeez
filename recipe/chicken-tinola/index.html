Chicken Tinola

A soothing ginger chicken soup with green papaya and leafy greens.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Sauté the ginger, garlic and onion.

  2. Add the chicken and fish sauce and cook for 5 minutes.

  3. Add the water and papaya and simmer for 20 minutes.

  4. Stir in the greens just before serving.

TipChayote works great if you can't find green papaya.
