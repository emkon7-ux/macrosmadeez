Egg White Breakfast Burrito

A breakfast burrito with 38g of protein for 330 calories. Make a few and freeze them.

High proteinBreakfast
330Calories
Protein
38g
Carbs
22g
Fat
10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1Low-carb tortilla
  • ¾ cupEgg whites
  • 2 ozTurkey sausage
  • 1 handfulSpinach
  • 2 tbspSalsa

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. First, brown the sausage.

  2. Add the spinach and scramble in the egg whites.

  3. Pile it into the tortilla with the salsa.

  4. Then roll it up and toast it seam side down.

Tiptoasting it seam side down keeps it from unrolling.
