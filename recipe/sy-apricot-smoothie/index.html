Qamar al-Din Smoothie

Apricot paste drink blended with yogurt.

Why it's good for you

  • Apricots are rich in vitamin A and fiber.
  • Natural sweetness, no added sugar.
  • Yogurt adds protein.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Tear the apricot leather and soak it in the water for 10 minutes.

  2. Blend with the yogurt and ice until smooth.

  3. Serve cold.
