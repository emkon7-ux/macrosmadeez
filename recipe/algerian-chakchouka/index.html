Chakchouka

Peppers and tomatoes with eggs, Algerian-style.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the sliced peppers in a little oil for 8 minutes until soft.

  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer 6 minutes.

  3. Make wells, add the eggs and pour in the whites.

  4. Cover and cook 5 minutes until set.

TipAdd cumin and paprika.
