Ropa Vieja

Cuban shredded beef with peppers.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the flank steak in salted water for 1 hour until tender.

  2. Shred it with two forks.

  3. Cook the sliced peppers and onion for 5 minutes, add the tomato and beef, and simmer 10 minutes.

  4. Serve with the black beans.
