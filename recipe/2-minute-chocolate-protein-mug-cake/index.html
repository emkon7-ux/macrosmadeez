2-Minute Chocolate Protein Mug Cake

Warm chocolate cake in two minutes with 25g of protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 scoopWhey protein
  • 1Egg white
  • 1 tbspCocoa powder
  • ½ tspBaking powder

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Mix it all in a mug with a splash of milk.

  2. Stir till there are no lumps.

  3. Then microwave it for about a minute.

Tippull it out a little early so the middle stays gooey.
