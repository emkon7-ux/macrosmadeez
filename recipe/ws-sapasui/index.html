Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soak the glass noodles in hot water 10 minutes.

  2. Stir-fry the sliced chicken in a little oil for 5 minutes.

  3. Add the vegetables, noodles and soy and toss 3 minutes.

  4. Season and serve.
