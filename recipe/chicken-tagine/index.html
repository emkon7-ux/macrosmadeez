Chicken Tagine with Lemon & Olives

A slow-cooked, fragrant chicken with preserved lemon, olives and chickpeas.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the spiced chicken.

  2. Soften the onion and garlic with the ras el hanout.

  3. Add the broth, chickpeas, lemon and olives, then the chicken.

  4. Cover and simmer gently for 35 minutes.

TipNo preserved lemon? Use the zest and juice of a fresh lemon plus a pinch of salt.
