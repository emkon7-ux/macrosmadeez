Azifa Lentil Salad

Green lentils dressed with mustard, lime and chili.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Rinse the cooked lentils and drain them well.

  2. Toss with diced red onion, chopped green chili, mustard and lime juice, then season with salt.

  3. Let it sit 10 minutes so the flavors soak in, and serve cold.
