Uzbek Plov

Uzbekistan's iconic rice with lamb, carrots and chickpeas.

    2Carrots, julienned

    1Garlic head

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the lamb in a heavy pot for 6 minutes.

  2. Add the carrots and a pinch of cumin and cook 5 minutes.

  3. Add the rinsed rice, chickpeas, the whole garlic head and 2/3 cup water.

  4. Cover and steam on low 25 minutes.

TipYellow carrots are traditional.
