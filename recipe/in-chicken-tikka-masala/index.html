Lighter Chicken Tikka Masala

Charred chicken in a creamy tomato sauce made with yogurt.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt and spice, then broil until charred.

  2. Simmer the tomato with spices.

  3. Stir in chicken and a spoon of yogurt off the heat.
