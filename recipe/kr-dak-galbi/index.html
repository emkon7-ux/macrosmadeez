Dakgalbi

Spicy stir-fried chicken with cabbage.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toss the cubed chicken with the gochujang, a little soy and garlic.

  2. Stir-fry with the sliced sweet potato for 8 minutes.

  3. Add the cabbage and cook 5 minutes.

  4. Serve when the sweet potato is tender.
