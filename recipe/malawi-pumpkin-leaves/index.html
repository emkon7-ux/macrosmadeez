Pumpkin Leaves in Peanut

Nkhwani greens with a peanut sauce.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Simmer the chopped greens in 1/2 cup water for 8 minutes.

  2. Add the diced tomato and cook 5 minutes.

  3. Whisk the peanut flour with a little water and stir it in.

  4. Simmer 5 minutes until creamy, season with salt and serve.
