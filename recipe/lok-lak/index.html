Beef Lok Lak

Peppery stir-fried beef on salad with lime-pepper dip.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cube the beef and marinate with a little soy, garlic and pepper for 10 minutes.

  2. Stir-fry over high heat 3 minutes.

  3. Serve on the lettuce and tomato with a fried egg.

  4. Mix lime juice with black pepper and salt for dipping.
