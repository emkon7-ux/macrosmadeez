Chicken Gyros Bowl

Chicken, tzatziki, salad and potatoes.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Rub the chicken with oregano, paprika, garlic and lemon, and roast with potato wedges at 425F for 25 minutes.

  2. Slice the chicken thin.

  3. Pile into a bowl with salad, potatoes and a big spoon of tzatziki.
