Fakes (Greek Lentil Soup)

Humble brown lentil soup with a splash of red wine vinegar at the end.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery.

  2. Add the lentils, tomato paste, bay leaves and water.

  3. Simmer for 30 minutes until creamy.

  4. Finish with the vinegar and a little olive oil.

TipThe vinegar at the end is non-negotiable. It wakes everything up.
