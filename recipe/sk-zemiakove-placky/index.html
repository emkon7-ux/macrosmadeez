Potato Placky

Garlic potato pancakes, baked.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Grate the potato and squeeze out the liquid.

  2. Mix with the egg, minced garlic and salt.

  3. Spoon onto a lined, oiled tray.

  4. Bake 20 minutes, flipping once.
