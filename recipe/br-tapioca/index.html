Tapioca Crepe with Eggs

Tapioca crepe filled with egg and cheese.

    1 ozMinas cheese (or queso fresco)

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Sift the hydrated tapioca flour into a dry nonstick pan in an even layer.

  2. Cook until it holds together, then flip.

  3. Fill with scrambled eggs and cheese and fold in half.
