Lighter Bandeja Paisa

Rice, beans, lean beef, egg and avocado.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a pan with salt and pepper, about 6 minutes.

  2. Warm the beans and rice.

  3. Fry the egg in a non-stick pan.

  4. Plate it all with the sliced avocado.
