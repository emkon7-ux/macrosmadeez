Why it's good for you

  • Basil seeds add fiber and keep you full.
  • Milk gives calcium.
  • Protein from yogurt.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Soak the seeds.

  2. Blend milk, yogurt and syrup.

  3. Stir in seeds.
