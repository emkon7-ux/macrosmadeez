Why it's good for you

  • Cashews add magnesium and healthy fats.
  • Cashew apple is very high in vitamin C.
  • Protein from yogurt.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Add the fruit, cashews and yogurt to a blender.

  2. Blend with ice until creamy.

  3. Serve with a few chopped cashews on top.
