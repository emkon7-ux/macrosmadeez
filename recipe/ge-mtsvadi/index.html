Mtsvadi

Georgian pork or beef skewers grilled over vine cuttings, served with raw onion and pomegranate.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Cube the meat and season with salt and pepper.

  2. Grill on skewers until charred outside and juicy inside.

  3. Serve with sliced onion and pomegranate.

TipA splash of pomegranate juice on top is traditional.
