Thieboudienne

Senegal's national dish of fish, tomato rice and vegetables.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Stuff the fish with the parsley-garlic paste.

  2. Simmer the veggies in tomato broth.

  3. Poach the fish, then cook the rice in the broth.

  4. Serve together.

TipThe crispy rice at the bottom is the prize.
