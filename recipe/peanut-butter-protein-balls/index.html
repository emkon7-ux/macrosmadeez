Peanut Butter Protein Balls

No-bake peanut butter protein balls. Makes 12, and 3 balls = 250 cal and 18g protein.

VegetarianSnack

Protein
18g

22g

10g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 cupRolled oats
  • ¼ cupPeanut butter
  • 1 scoopVanilla whey
  • 1 tbspHoney

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Mix everything in a bowl.

  2. Add a splash of milk if it's dry.

  3. Roll it into bite size balls.

  4. Then chill them for thirty minutes.

Tipwet your hands a little so the mix doesn't stick while rolling.
