Egg Roll in a Bowl

The inside of an egg roll, in a bowl. One pan and 35g of protein.

High proteinLunch & dinner
320Calories
Protein
35g
Carbs
14g
Fat
13g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozLean ground turkey
  • 2 cupsColeslaw mix
  • 1 tspFresh ginger
  • 1 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
  • 1Green onion

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the turkey with the ginger.

  2. Toss in the coleslaw mix.

  3. Add the soy sauce and cook till it softens.

  4. Then top with green onion.

Tipa drizzle of sesame oil at the end makes it taste like takeout.
