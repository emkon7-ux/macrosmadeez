Chicken Pesto Zucchini Boats

Low-carb zucchini boats packed with pesto chicken and melty mozzarella.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerItaly
350Calories

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 2Zucchini, halved
  • 5 ozCooked chicken, diced
  • 1 tbspLight pesto
  • ¼ cupPart-skim mozzarella

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Slice the zucchini in half and scoop out the middle.

  2. Mix the chicken with the pesto.

  3. Fill the boats and top with mozzarella.

  4. Then bake at 400 for fifteen minutes.

Tipsalt the zucchini and let it sit 10 minutes so the boats aren't watery.
