Kudra Beef Stew

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with the onion in a pot.

  2. Add the tomato and 2 cups of water and simmer 30 minutes until tender.

  3. Stir in the greens and peanut butter and cook 5 minutes more until creamy.
