Turkey Meatballs & Zoodles

Spaghetti and meatballs energy for 380 calories and 40g protein.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 ozLean turkey meatballs
  • ½ cupMarinara
  • 2Zucchini, spiralized
  • A few leavesFresh basil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Bake the meatballs at 400 for fifteen minutes.

  2. Warm the marinara and drop them in.

  3. Sauté the zoodles for just a minute.

  4. Then pile it up and top with basil.

Tipsalt the zoodles and pat them dry so the sauce doesn't get watery.
