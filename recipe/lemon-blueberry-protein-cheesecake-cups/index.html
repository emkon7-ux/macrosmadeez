Lemon Blueberry Protein Cheesecake Cups

No-bake cheesecake cups made with blended cottage cheese. Makes 2 cups.

VegetarianDessert
250Calories
Protein
25g
Carbs
22g
Fat
6g

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1 cupCottage cheese
  • ½ scoopVanilla whey
  • 1Lemon
  • ½ cupBlueberries
  • 1 sheetGraham crackers

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Blend the cottage cheese, whey and lemon till silky.

  2. Crumble the graham cracker into a glass.

  3. Spoon the filling on top.

  4. Then finish with blueberries.

Tipblend for a full minute so it's completely smooth.
