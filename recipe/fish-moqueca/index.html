Fish Moqueca

A Bahian coconut fish stew with peppers, tomato and lime.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Marinate the fish in the lime, garlic and salt.

  2. Layer the onion, peppers and tomato in a pan.

  3. Nestle in the fish and pour over the coconut milk.

  4. Simmer covered for 12 minutes, then add cilantro.

TipA teaspoon of red palm oil (dendê) makes it taste authentic.
