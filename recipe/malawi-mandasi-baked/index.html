Baked Mandasi

Lightly sweet baked doughnuts.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.

  2. Mix the flour, egg, sugar, cardamom, a pinch of baking powder and a little milk into a soft dough.

  3. Shape into small balls.

  4. Bake 15 minutes until golden.
