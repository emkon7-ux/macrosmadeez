Bobó de Camarão

Shrimp in creamy cassava and coconut.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Boil the cassava until soft and blend with coconut milk.

  2. Cook the shrimp with tomato and onion.

  3. Stir in the cassava cream and finish with cilantro.
