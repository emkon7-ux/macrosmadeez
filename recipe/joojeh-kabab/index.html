Joojeh Kabab

Saffron-yogurt chicken kebabs with rice.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Bloom the saffron in 1 tbsp hot water and mix with the yogurt, lemon juice, grated onion and salt.

  2. Marinate the cubed chicken at least 30 minutes, then skewer.

  3. Grill 10 to 12 minutes, turning.

  4. Serve with basmati rice.

TipMarinate for at least 2 hours.
