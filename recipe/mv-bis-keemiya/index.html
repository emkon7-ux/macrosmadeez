Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Cook the shredded cabbage for 5 minutes and mix with the tuna and salt.

  2. Fill squares of dough and fold into triangles.

  3. Place on a lined tray.

  4. Bake 15 minutes until golden.
