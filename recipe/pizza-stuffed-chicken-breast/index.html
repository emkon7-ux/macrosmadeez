Pizza Stuffed Chicken Breast

Chicken breast stuffed with all your favorite pizza toppings. Almost no carbs.

High proteinGluten-freeLunch & dinnerItaly

Protein

Per serving. Macros are approximate.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 6 ozChicken breast
  • 2 tbspMarinara
  • ¼ cupPart-skim mozzarella
  • 8 slicesTurkey pepperoni

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Slice a pocket into the chicken.

  2. Stuff it with mozzarella and pepperoni.

  3. Top it with marinara and a little more cheese.

  4. Then bake at 400 for twenty minutes.

Tipsecure the pocket with a toothpick so the cheese stays in.
