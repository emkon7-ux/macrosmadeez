Buckwheat Kasha with Chicken

Toasted buckwheat with mushrooms and chicken.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Toast the buckwheat in a dry pan for 3 minutes.

  2. Add 2/3 cup broth, cover and simmer 15 minutes.

  3. Sauté the diced onion, sliced mushrooms and cubed chicken for 10 minutes.

  4. Stir into the buckwheat and season.

TipBuckwheat is gluten-free despite the name.
