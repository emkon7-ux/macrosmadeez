Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the minced garlic and diced onion in a little oil for 4 minutes.

  2. Add the chopped tomato and cook 4 minutes.

  3. Add the scrubbed clams, cover and steam 5 minutes until they open.

  4. Discard any that stay closed and serve.
