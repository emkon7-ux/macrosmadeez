Why it's good for you

  • Dates give fast natural energy, fiber and potassium.
  • Tahini adds calcium, iron and healthy fats.
  • With yogurt it has about 24g of protein for recovery.

General nutrition info, not medical advice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Pit the dates and soak them in the milk for 5 minutes to soften.

  2. Add the tahini, Greek yogurt and a handful of ice and blend until smooth.

  3. Serve with a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
