Koki (Steamed Bean Cakes)

Steamed black-eyed pea cakes with palm oil.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    3/4 cupBlack-eyed peas
  • 2
    1 tspPalm oil
  • 3
    1Scotch bonnet pepper (use half for less heat)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soak the peas overnight, rub off the skins and blend smooth with a little water.

  2. Stir in the palm oil, chopped pepper and salt.

  3. Pour into greased cups and cover with foil.

  4. Steam 45 minutes until firm.
