Baked Chicken Empanadas

Two golden baked empanadas filled with spiced chicken, peppers and olives.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    4 ozCooked chicken, diced
  • 2
    2Empanada discs
  • 3
    1/4Onion & bell pepper
  • 4
    4Green olives, chopped
  • 5
    1 tspCumin & paprika
  • 6
    1Egg (for wash)

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the onion and pepper with the spices.

  2. Stir in the chicken and olives and let it cool.

  3. Fill the discs, fold and crimp.

  4. Brush with egg and bake at 400°F for 18 minutes.

TipChill the filling before you fill them so the dough stays crisp.
