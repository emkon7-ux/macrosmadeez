Sukuma Wiki with Eggs

Garlicky sautéed collard greens with tomatoes and scrambled eggs.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the onion and tomato.

  2. Add the greens and cook for 5 minutes.

  3. Push to one side and scramble the eggs.

  4. Toss together and season.

TipSlice the greens very thin so they cook fast.
