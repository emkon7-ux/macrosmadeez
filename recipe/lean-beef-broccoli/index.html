Lean Beef & Broccoli

Takeout-style beef and broccoli with a glossy sauce, 40g protein and a fraction of the sodium.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 5 oz93% lean flank, thin strips
  • 2 cupsBroccoli
  • 2 tbspLow-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tspFresh ginger, grated
  • 1 tspCornstarch + water

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Sear the beef in a hot pan, then set it aside.

  2. Steam the broccoli till it's bright green.

  3. Add the soy, ginger and slurry.

  4. Then toss the beef back in till it's glossy.

Tipslice the beef against the grain so it stays tender.
