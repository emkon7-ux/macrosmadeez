Baasto iyo Suugo

Somali pasta with spiced beef sauce and banana on the side.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Brown the beef with onion, garlic and xawaash.

  2. Add the tomato sauce and simmer 15 minutes while the spaghetti cooks.

  3. Toss the pasta in the sauce and serve with a banana on the side.

TipThe banana is non-negotiable.
