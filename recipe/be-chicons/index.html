Endive Gratin (Lighter)

Endives wrapped in ham, baked.

1 serving

  • 1
    2Endives
  • 2
    2 slicesLean ham
  • 3
    1 ozGouda
  • 4
    1/4 cupLow-fat (1%) milk

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Halve the endives and steam 8 minutes.

  2. Wrap each half in ham and lay in a small dish.

  3. Warm the milk with the grated Gouda and pour over.

  4. Bake 15 minutes until bubbling.
