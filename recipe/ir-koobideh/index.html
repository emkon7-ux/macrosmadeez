Kabab Koobideh

Ground beef kebab with grilled tomato and rice.

Steps 3 easy steps

  1. Squeeze the grated onion and mix it into the beef with salt.

  2. Press onto flat skewers and grill with the tomato.

  3. Serve with saffron rice and a dusting of sumac.
