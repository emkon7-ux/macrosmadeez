Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Brown the seasoned chicken in a pot for 4 minutes per side.

  2. Add the mushrooms and onions and cook 5 minutes.

  3. Pour in the wine and 1/4 cup water.

  4. Braise covered 45 minutes.
