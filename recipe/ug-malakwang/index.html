Malakwang with Fish

Sour greens in peanut sauce with fish.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Boil the greens 10 minutes and drain.

  2. Stir in the peanut paste with a little water.

  3. Simmer 5 minutes.

  4. Serve with the grilled fish.
