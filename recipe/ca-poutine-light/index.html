Lighter Poutine

Baked fries, cheese curds and light gravy.

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cut the potato into fries and bake at 425°F for 25 minutes.

  2. Warm the gravy.

  3. Top the fries with the curds and pour over the hot gravy.

  4. Add the sliced cooked chicken.
