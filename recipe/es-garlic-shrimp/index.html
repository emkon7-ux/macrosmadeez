Gambas al Ajillo

Garlic shrimp with chili.

1 serving

    7 ozLarge raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2
    4Garlic
  • 3
    1Fresh red chili (Fresno or long red)
  • 4
    1 tspOlive oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Warm the olive oil with the sliced garlic and chili over low heat for 2 minutes without browning.

  2. Turn the heat to medium-high and add the shrimp with a pinch of salt.

  3. Cook 3 minutes, turning once, until pink.

  4. Serve with the garlicky oil.
