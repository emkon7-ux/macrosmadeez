Dushbara

Tiny lamb dumplings in broth.

1 serving

  • 2
    20Mini wrappers
  • 3
    2 cupsLow-sodium beef broth

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Season the lamb and put a tiny bit in each mini wrapper; pinch closed.

  2. Bring the broth to a simmer.

  3. Add the dumplings and cook 8 minutes.

  4. Sprinkle with dried mint.
