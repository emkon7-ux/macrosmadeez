Spanish Tortilla (Lighter)

A thick potato and onion omelet with extra egg whites for protein.

VegetarianGluten-freeBreakfastSpain30 min

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    2Eggs
  • 2
    3/4 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs
  • 3
    5 ozPotato, thinly sliced
  • 4
    1/2Onion, sliced
  • 5
    1 tspOlive oil

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Cook the potato and onion slowly in the oil until tender.

  2. Whisk the eggs and whites with salt.

  3. Pour over the potatoes and cook gently.

  4. Flip with a plate and cook the other side.

TipMicrowave the potato slices for 3 minutes first to cut the cooking time.
