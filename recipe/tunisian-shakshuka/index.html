Tunisian Shakshuka

Eggs poached in a harissa tomato and pepper sauce.

Ingredients Tap to check off

1 serving

  • 1
    2Eggs
  • 2
    1/2 cupLiquid egg whites (carton) or whites from large eggs
  • 3
    1 cupCrushed tomatoes
  • 4
    1Red pepper, sliced
  • 5
    1 tspHarissa
  • 6
    1 tspCumin

Steps 4 easy steps

  1. Soften the peppers with garlic.

  2. Add the tomatoes, harissa and cumin and simmer for 8 minutes.

  3. Pour in the whites, then crack in the eggs.

  4. Cover until the eggs are just set.

TipHarissa varies a lot in heat, so start with half a teaspoon.
